Guillermo Rauch CEO, Vercel

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch introduces 𝚡𝚎𝚎𝚝, a hermetic, pixel-perfect X post renderer in safe Rust/wasm that needs no browser, network, filesystem or system fonts and renders a post to PNG in about 1ms (0.4ms for jack's 'setting up my twttr'), which he says is 100x-1000x faster than booting a browser for a screenshot at the same accuracy.
