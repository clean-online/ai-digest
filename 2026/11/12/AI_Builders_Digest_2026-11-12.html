Designing for Agents — Zeno Rocha, Resend

The Takeaway: a product that language models keep recommending got there not by buying an SEO inheritance but by rewriting every page into a form an agent can actually read, from FAQs and llms.txt to a plain-text version of the pricing page. Zeno Rocha, founder of Resend and author of the Dracula theme and React Email, came to email as a front-end developer with no infrastructure background, on the premise that a large, old market had plenty of competitors but nobody caring about the developer persona, and asked what the Stripe of email would look like. He reports 4.3 million users as of that morning, 120,000 paid customers, a ten million AR company, and customers including Warner Brothers and HBO; the SDK's NPM downloads crossed SendGrid's earlier this year and are now more than double, though the volume of mail sent is still much smaller than SendGrid's. The transcript offers no speaker labels and no timestamps, and every figure in it is a self-report that the feed does not verify.

The part most builders can steal is the delivery mechanics. Volume email is not an app you scale by adding GPUs: Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo each behave differently, so 100,000 messages cannot simply be thrown at Gmail at once. The engaged recipients go first in a queue, batches are spaced out, IP blocks are bought with their history checked and balanced into pools over time. The rule is to do exactly what an abuser would not do, since an abuser buys a fresh domain, sends a lot at once and never sends again. Resend says it trusts users by default instead of putting everyone behind a two-day human review, then blocks quickly on bounce and complaint signals and talks to the customer, because most cases turn out to be misuse rather than abuse, a skill issue. Auto-generating a plain-text version of every HTML email raised deliverability, because spam filters can read the content directly instead of parsing it. Open rates are no longer reliable after Apple's privacy changes, and click tracking that rewrites links to a shared domain risks a noisy-neighbor blacklist.

The agent-facing work is specific and deliberately unglamorous. Resend writes long-tail docs for every framework, puts an FAQ on almost every page, adds llms.txt on docs and marketing pages, and serves a markdown version of every page by appending .md or by content negotiation. The pricing page mattered: a model once answered a cost comparison with wrong numbers and called the pricing obscure, so he published a plain-text version, because agents do not really drag a JavaScript widget, and a few days later the same question came back right. He is openly unsure which of these moves help, saying of the markdown trick that he is not sure it matters, and he describes a recency bias in at least Opus 4.5 that favoured newer tools with more organic coverage. On MCP he reports at least two X growth month over month, around 1.2 million calls by the end of August by his own uncertain estimate, and more than 80 tools now against roughly 20 or fewer at launch, explicitly against Cloudflare's two; the first MCP was an open-source local one built by Yoko, an a16z investor, and doing a remote MCP properly meant rebuilding the permissions model through OAuth and RBAC. As a buyer he dropped Plausible for PostHog because the former's MCP was built by Sentry rather than maintained by the vendor, and uses Strava because Strava has an MCP. The most human line is about scale and priority: he says he would rather be a ten million AR company whose daughter wants to play with him than a hundred million dollar one where she does not, after a YC partner told him he worried too much about design. Whether any single AEO tactic works is left open; the conversation separately mentions four analytics subscriptions and a home-made AEO tracker, with no result attached to either.

核心结论：一个被语言模型反复推荐的产品，靠的不是继承一份 SEO 老本，而是把每个页面都改写成 agent 真能读的形式，从 FAQ、llms.txt 到定价页的纯文本版本。Zeno Rocha 是 Resend 创始人，也是 Dracula 主题和 React Email 的作者，他以前端开发者、毫无基础设施背景的身份进入邮件这个领域，前提是这个又大又老的赛道竞争者很多，却没人在乎开发者这个角色，于是他问：如果这是「邮件里的 Stripe」，该长什么样。他报出的数字是当天早上 430 万用户、12 万付费客户、一家 1000 万美元 ARR 的公司，客户里有 Warner Brothers 和 HBO；SDK 的 NPM 下载量今年早些时候超过 SendGrid，现在是其两倍多，不过实际发信量仍远小于 SendGrid。转写没有 speaker 标签、没有时间戳，里面每个数字都是自述，feed 没有核实其中任何一项。

最值得搬走的是投递机制。批量邮件不是加 GPU 就能扩容的 app：Gmail、Outlook、Yahoo 各家规则不同，10 万封不能一次砸给 Gmail。要把互动最活跃的收件人排在队列前面，分批、批次之间留间隔，买 IP 段时查历史、再分散成 IP 池长期做平衡。原则是「滥用者怎么做，你就反着来」：滥用者买个新域名、一次性狂发、之后再也不发。Resend 说他们默认信任用户，不把所有人挡在两天的审核后面，而是靠退信和投诉信号快速封禁、再去找客户聊，因为多数情况是使用不当而不是恶意滥用，是 skill issue。给每封 HTML 邮件自动生成纯文本版本提升了送达率，因为垃圾邮件过滤器可以直接读内容而不用去解析。Apple 的隐私改动之后 open rate 不再可靠；把链接重写到共享域名的 click tracking 则有被邻居拖进黑名单的风险。

面向 agent 的活很具体，也刻意不漂亮。Resend 给每个框架写长尾文档，几乎每页都加 FAQ，在文档和营销页都放 llms.txt，并且给每个页面提供 markdown 版本，追加 .md 或走内容协商都能拿到。定价页是个转折点：有一次模型算比价给出错误数字、还说它的定价含糊，于是他补了一版纯文本，因为 agent 不会去拖一个 JavaScript 组件；几天后再问同一个问题，答案就对了。他坦白说不清哪招真的有用，谈到 markdown 那招时说 not sure it matters；他说至少在 Opus 4.5 上能看到一种近因偏差，更偏爱较新、自然被讨论得更多的工具。MCP 方面他报的是每月至少两倍增长、按他自己带不确定语气的估计到 8 月底约 120 万次调用、现在 80 多个工具而上线时大约 20 个或更少，并明确拿来对比 Cloudflare 只有两个；第一个 MCP 是 a16z 投资人 Yoko 做的开源本地版本，而把 remote MCP 做对，意味着把权限模型经 OAuth 和 RBAC 重做一遍。作为买方，他因为 Plausible 的 MCP 由 Sentry 代做、不是厂商自己维护而换用 PostHog，用 Strava 则因为 Strava 有 MCP。最有人味的一句关于规模与取舍：当年 YC 一位合伙人说他太在意设计，他后来的结论是，宁可做一家 1000 万美元 ARR、女儿还愿意跟他玩的公司，也不做一家一亿美元、女儿不想理他的公司。至于某一招 AEO 到底有没有用，对话里没有下结论；另外提到四份分析工具订阅和一个自制的 AEO tracker，都没有给出结果。
