Training Data
A Model For Everyone: How Ronak From Trajectory Is Building Products For Continual Learning

The Takeaway: what AI is missing is a way for each company to feed its own real-world experience back into a model, cheaply and continuously. Ronak, who builds Trajectory, starts from an Apple analogy. In the early 2000s a computer had the Internet and a phone had calls, and nobody thought to put the two together; his bet is that training, eval and deployment belong in one place, so a model can be uploaded back to production, gather new traces and close the loop. He calls today's setup 'pseudo continual learning': agents are like AAA paper maps or MapQuest, where any route change means a full batch update, while the goal is Google Maps, where every drive updates the map. Humans, he says, are the only true continual learners.

核心结论：AI 现在缺的，是让每家公司把自己真实场景里的经验低成本、持续地回灌进模型的一条路。Ronak 在做的 Trajectory 从苹果的类比讲起。2000 年初，电脑能上网、手机能打电话，没人想到把两者合到一起；他的判断是训练、评估、部署应该放在同一个地方，模型传回生产环境、收集新的 traces，闭环就合上了。他把今天的状态叫「伪持续学习」：现在的 agent 像 AAA 的纸质地图或 MapQuest，改一次路线就要整批重算，目标则是 Google Maps，每开一次车地图都在更新。他说人类才是真正的持续学习者。

The concrete claim is about cost. Training a custom model used to need 'hundreds of millions of dollars of R and D in compute', a large research team and six to nine months, as he describes from his time at Windsurf and from Cursor Composer. Trajectory's customers bring agent traces and a definition of 'what good looks like', and get a one-click train, eval and deploy loop. With Harvey, he reports a model 'six times cheaper' that moved accuracy 'from around 4% accuracy for an open source model to around 18%', and says cheaper models let you 'expend so many more tokens'. With Rogo, the target is what he calls 'intelligence density': the same task, far fewer tokens.

更具体的一层是成本。按他在 Windsurf 以及 Cursor Composer 时期的描述，训一个定制模型过去要「数亿美元的研发算力」、一个庞大的研究团队和六到九个月。Trajectory 的客户只需带来 agent traces 和一份「什么算好」的定义，就能走一键训练、评估、部署的闭环。Harvey 那一例，他报的是模型「便宜六倍」，准确率「从开源模型约 4% 提到约 18%」，并说更便宜的模型让你「能花得起多得多的 token」。Rogo 那一例，目标是他说的「智能密度」：同样的任务，用少得多的 token 做完。

Two research problems remain. Environments are hard to reproduce, so his team pushes gRPO toward 'group size one', learning from 10,000 trajectories that each come from a different task instead of replaying one environment eight or 16 times. Rewards are hard to label, so they pick 100 representative traces, have the customer write a rubric for those, and train a meta grader to propagate it to the wider 10,000. His longer-range word for what is still missing is curiosity: LLMs go 'straight from state zero to state one' but do not encode the transition function in between.

还有两个研究上的难点。环境很难复现，所以团队把 gRPO 往「group size one」推，让 10,000 条各来自不同任务的 trajectory 也能学习，而不是把同一个环境重放 8 次或 16 次。奖励很难标注，于是挑 100 条有代表性的 trace，让客户只为这 100 条写评分标准，再训一个 meta grader 把标准推广到全部 10,000 条。他把更远处仍缺的东西叫好奇心：LLM 会「直接从状态零跳到状态一」，但中间那段转换函数没有被编码进去。

He draws a line between recursive self-improvement, which puts a model in a black box to improve itself, and continual learning, which 'keeps humans in the loop'. He contrasts today's 'pre industrial revolution society', where everyone consults 'one oracle of technology', with Adam Smith's specialisation in Wealth of Nations. His pitch for product builders is that they become 'almost like a gardener', setting trellises and letting the product grow. 'There's a reason why only 1% of enterprises actually have been adopted by AI,' he says; the goal is to turn that 1% into 99%.

他划了一条线：递归自我改进是把模型放进黑盒让它自我提升，持续学习则「让人留在回路里」。他把今天比作「工业革命前的社会」，人人都去问「一个技术神谕」，对照的是《国富论》里亚当·斯密讲的专业化。给产品人的说法是，产品建造者会变得「几乎像个园丁」，搭好棚架，让产品自己长。「只有 1% 的企业真正用上了 AI，这是有原因的，」他说，目标是把这 1% 变成 99%。
