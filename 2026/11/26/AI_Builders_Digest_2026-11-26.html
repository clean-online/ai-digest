Ep 98: China Expert on Jensen’s Return

The Takeaway: when one side holds a ten to fifteen fold compute advantage, co-designing models with your own chips barely moves the number; the real leverage sits in the manufacturing equipment and materials that decide who can build at scale.

Jordan Schneider has hosted China Talk for ten years. He joined Jacob Efron, an investor at Redpoint, for an emergency episode on Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia. Much of the conversation is about export controls, compute and manufacturing, with the AI industry tied to industrial policy.

On compute, Schneider's argument is that the western ecosystem's ten to fifteen times advantage makes model and chip co-development a rounding error. He says the constraint on Huawei is supply, not demand: high-bandwidth memory and the EUV, etch and deposition machines needed to make advanced chips. Controls on those tools, he argues, matter more than controls on finished Nvidia chips.

On robots, he describes walking the floor at CORAL, a robot machine learning conference in Austin, and finding the humanoids unable to do much. A DeepMind demo could barely pick up a muffin. On that split he says: 'the model thing is 20%, and then it's gonna be all this other stuff,' from actuators to magnets, with many components still sourced from a single place. He puts the odds of new controls on robotics data at about 75 percent.

On public support, he cites a poll in which only 2 percent of Americans want AI to progress faster, and warns that data centre backlash could turn into state level and eventually national legislation within a few years. Names, figures and spellings follow the transcript; this is an interview and its claims are the speakers'.

核心结论：当一方握有十到十五倍的算力优势，让模型和自研芯片共同设计几乎改变不了这个数字；真正的杠杆在决定谁能规模化制造的设备和材料上。

Jordan Schneider 主持 China Talk 已有十年。他做客 Jacob Efron（Redpoint 投资人）的一期临时节目，谈 Jensen Huang 回到 Nvidia。整场对话把 AI 产业和产业政策连在一起看，大部分围绕出口管制、算力和制造。

算力方面，Schneider 的说法是西方生态十到十五倍的优势，让模型和芯片的共同优化变成一个舍入误差。他说华为的瓶颈在供给而非需求：高带宽内存，以及制造先进芯片需要的 EUV、刻蚀和沉积设备。他认为管制这些设备，比管制成品 Nvidia 芯片更关键。

机器人方面，他描述在奥斯汀的机器人机器学习会议 CORAL 逛展，看到那些人形机器人基本做不了什么；DeepMind 的演示勉强能拿起一个松饼。谈到两者分工，他说：「模型的部分占 20%，剩下的全是别的东西」，从执行器到磁体，很多部件仍来自单一来源。他给「机器人数据会被加管制」这件事约 75% 的概率。

公众支持度上，他引用一份民调，说只有 2% 的美国人希望 AI 更快推进，并提醒数据中心的反弹可能在几年内变成州层面、最终是全国层面的立法。人名、数字和拼写按转写原样照录；这是一场访谈，其中的说法都属于说话人本人。
