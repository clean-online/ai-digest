Latent Space
Scaling Linear’s GTM to $100M ARR — Cristina Cordova, COO

The Takeaway: scaling a product-led company into enterprise is less about adding a sales playbook than about subtracting the goals you cannot yet resource. Christina Cordova (spelled Cristina Cordova in the episode title) has been COO of Linear for about three and a half years, after leading platform, APIs, partnerships and self-serve growth at Notion and seven and a half years at Stripe across early partnerships, go-to-market and product. She is skeptical of her own job title: when founders ask how to scale the business side after product-market fit, she often cautions against hiring someone like her, because early on what a company usually needs is simply someone who can sell.

Three weeks after joining, at an offsite in front of 35-40 people, she crossed out one of Linear's three company-level goals, 'move up market to enterprise', with the line 'We have literally one person on sales.' Linear's customers at that point were self-serve signups that grew into larger accounts: Vercel and Ramp, she says, never talked to a human being at Linear. Selling to a company that will not put a lick of data into your service without a contract, a pilot and a survey of what tool the team used before is a different motion entirely. She says the company has at least 10Xed since she joined.

On winning customers early, her bar is being significantly better, not 20% or 30% better, because switching costs eat small gains; the Linear founders had used the legacy tools at Coinbase, Airbnb and Uber and knew nobody liked them. Marketing was initially just changelog emails, but less than 10% of users subscribed, so the team moved release notes into the product where people already spent their days, plus a company tweet the founders would reshare. When Linear did hire, it optimized for AEs who had sold technical products to designers and engineers, on the logic that you would not have to teach that discernment; it does not do forward deployed engineering, and added solutions engineers later.

On pricing she is concrete: Linear has 40,000 paying customers and was an entirely seat-based business until June, which makes growing from zero to 100 million overnight much harder than for a business that signs a few 30-to-40-million-dollar ARR deals with defense or gigantic law firms. Some features are now usage-based, but pricing on the outcome you drive is hard outside the Intercom Fin model of a dollar per AI-answered support ticket. What is automatically triaging a bug to the right team worth? Her read on AI companies getting to $100 million of revenue within three months is that they unlocked budget that previously did not exist, from buyers afraid the company will die if it does not experiment; those are one-year deals and pilots, not three-year contracts, and she expects the experimenting to stop in a year or two once buyers work out what actually works.

The org stays deliberately thin: Linear has no C-suite beyond the founders and her, and at about 170 people wants to remain pretty flat, using head-of titles over wide areas of ownership rather than adding layers. She also invests, and says the useful return is sitting in a board meeting knowing what data, metrics and introductions investors expect. 'closed mouths don't get fed', as she puts it, so you have to ask. The cold open has a Linear blog post declaring issue tracking is dead and customers asking what they are buying; the opening introduces the hosts and guest and the closing read is a subscription promo, none treated as an argument. Names, spellings and figures are kept as the transcript gives them; the feed does not independently verify them.

核心结论：把一家产品驱动的公司做成企业级生意，关键不在加一套销售打法，而在先把当下根本没资源兑现的目标划掉。转写里写作 Christina Cordova、节目标题里写作 Cristina Cordova，她在 Linear 做 COO 约三年半；此前在 Notion 负责 platform、API、合作与自助增长，再之前在 Stripe 待了七年半，做过早期合作、go-to-market，后来转产品侧。她对自己这个头衔是怀疑的：创始人问「找到产品市场契合之后，业务侧怎么放大」，她常劝别急着招「像我这样的人」，因为早期真正缺的往往只是有人能把东西卖出去。

加入三周后的公司 offsite，她当着 35 到 40 人的面，把 Linear 三个公司级目标里的「move up market to enterprise」划掉了，理由是 'We have literally one person on sales.' 那时 Linear 的客户基本是自助注册、跟着公司一起长大的，她说 Vercel、Ramp 这类客户从来没跟 Linear 的人说过话；而卖给一家「不先签合同、不做 pilot、不调研团队原来用什么工具，就一条数据都不肯放进来」的公司，是完全不同的动作。她说自己加入以来公司至少 10X。
