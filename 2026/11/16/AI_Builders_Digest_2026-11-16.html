Aaron Levie CEO, Box

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that as models keep getting better at coding, tool and computer use, math and other coding-related tasks, agents that use software to do their work or generate code from scratch will improve faster than all other domains of knowledge work. His conclusion is that more and more work will just become 'coding-shaped' to adapt to what agents are good at, visible in recent releases where agents generate code that becomes animated videos, games and 3D models. He says models do not really have to know everything about anything; they have to use or generate software effectively, combined with deep domain expertise to make the right judgment calls inside workflows, and that this combination unlocks almost everything else (206 likes, 96 replies, third in replies).

Box CEO Aaron Levie 的判断是：模型在编码、工具与计算机使用、数学等编码相关任务上会持续变强，因此能用软件干活、或从零生成代码的 agent，进步速度会快于知识工作的其他领域。他的结论是，越来越多的工作会变成「coding-shaped」，去适配 agent 擅长的事，近几个月的模型发布里已经能看到 agent 生成代码再变成动画视频、游戏和 3D 模型。他说模型其实不必什么都知道，真正要做的是有效地使用或生成软件，再叠加深度领域专业知识，在工作流里做对判断；有了这个组合，其余几乎都会被解锁（206 赞、96 回复，回复第三）。
