Madhu Guru Sr Director, AI at Meta (ex-Gemini/Veo)

Continued his 'how to get good at evals' series with Part 3: the failure-mode taxonomy. Once you have v1 evals, mine your last 500-1,000 production traces, cluster the failures, and name them specifically - 'wrong document retrieved,' 'failed to ground and hallucinated,' 'made poor assumptions instead of asking.' Precise failure names become targeted eval tests, which closes the loop into an improvement flywheel.

在他'如何把 evals 做扎实'系列里更新了第 3 篇：失败模式分类法。有了 v1 版 evals 之后，去翻最近 500-1000 条生产轨迹，把失败聚类并精确命名——'检索到了错误的文档''没接地就幻觉''不追问而是做了糟糕假设'。精确的失败命名会直接变成针对性的 eval 用例，从而把 evals 接到改进飞轮上。

Aaron Levie Box CEO

Made the case that in the AI era, experts still have the upper hand - and it's accelerating. AI makes starting any task 10x easier (coding, legal, research, finance), but directing the agent, course-correcting, reviewing output, and knowing what 'good' looks like all demand field-specific skill. His sharper claim: AI widens the gap between skill levels, because experts now have more leverage than ever - so don't abandon being an expert. Separately, on the Stripe + OpenRouter deal: broad AI diffusion needs seamless mixing of intelligence from many providers and better cost management.

他的核心判断是：在 AI 时代，专家依然占上风，而且这一趋势还在加速。AI 让任何任务的起步都便宜了 10 倍（编码、法律、研究、金融），但如何给 agent 指方向、纠偏、审稿、以及对'好'有判断力，都极度依赖垂直领域的真功夫。更尖锐的一句是：AI 会拉大技能差距，因为专家比以往拥有更高的杠杆——所以别放弃成为专家。另外，关于 Stripe + OpenRouter 的合作：AI 要更广泛地扩散，开发者与企业需要能无缝混用多家智能、并更好地管理成本。

Guillermo Rauch Vercel CEO

Showcased fx - a 6.3MB binary (even smaller as a WebAssembly build) that boots in about 10 microseconds, compiled in Zig as a static ELF. His thesis: AI will make most infrastructure natively optimized, and 'fx can literally finish tasks faster than other agents can boot.' Fast, he argues, is a one-way street.

他展示了 fx——一个 6.3MB 的二进制（WebAssembly 版本更小），启动仅需约 10 微秒，用 Zig 编译成静态 ELF。他的论点是：AI 会让大部分基础设施变成'原生即优化'，而'fx 完成任务的速度，真的快过别的 agent 启动的速度'。快，在他看来是一条单行道。

Amjad Masad Replit CEO

Announced a partnership with OpenAI and framed the thesis plainly: agents made software cheaper but made coding more expensive - and together they're changing that. The implication is a push to bring the cost of actually producing code back down as agentic workflows mature.

他宣布了与 OpenAI 的合作，并把观点说得很直白：agent 让软件更便宜，却让'写代码'这件事更贵了——而现在他们要一起改变这一点。潜台词是：随着 agent 工作流成熟，要把真正动手产出代码的成本重新压下来。

Thariq Claude Code, Anthropic (ex YC W20)

Praised a new AI-native game - one of the first to genuinely use AI capabilities to unlock new gameplay - saying he'd urged Sam to ship it last summer. On the bigger thesis: software creation has always been unreliable (late, over budget, missing user needs), which is exactly why the 'software factory' matters - for non-software companies, software must become a reliable, predictable process, while building net-new products stays risky but profitable.

他力挺一款真正用 AI 能力解锁新玩法的游戏——属于最早一批靠 AI 原生玩法立住的作品，并说自己去年夏天就催 Sam 把它发出来。更大的判断是：软件创造从来都是不可靠的（延期、超预算、对不准需求），这恰恰就是'软件工厂'的意义所在——对非软件公司而言，软件必须变成可靠、可预测的流程，而做全新的软件产品则依然高风险但高回报。

Thibault Sottiaux Codex & ChatGPT, OpenAI

Previewed Private Safety Processing, a system built to keep offering Zero Data Retention (ZDR) while strengthening safeguards: for ZDR deployments, content stays on customer-controlled infrastructure, and automated systems return only limited safety signals without exposing prompts or responses to OpenAI staff. A customer-key-encrypted, OpenAI-hosted option is in early testing, with rollout planned for September. Separately, a glimpse of 'Codex for scale' - a compute allocation for Codex that's been provisioned but not yet used.

他预告了 Private Safety Processing（私密安全处理）：在保持零数据保留（ZDR）的同时强化护栏。在 ZDR 部署下，内容留在客户自有的基础设施上，自动化系统只回传有限的安全信号，不会把用户的 prompt 或回答暴露给 OpenAI 员工。一个由客户密钥加密、OpenAI 托管的版本正在早期测试中，计划 9 月逐步推出。另外还露出了一瞥'Codex for scale'——为 Codex 预留、尚未动用的算力额度。

Peter Yang Practical AI tutorials & interviews

Shared a personal essay on using AI to navigate the healthcare system alongside his mother's battle with breast cancer, framed as a practical guide for patients. It's a concrete, human use case for AI beyond productivity - sitting with someone through diagnosis, treatment options, and the maze of care.

他分享了一篇个人文章，讲述在母亲与乳腺癌斗争的过程中，如何用 AI 来穿越繁杂的医疗系统，并把它当作一份给患者用的实操指南。这是一个超出'提效'范畴、非常具体而有人情味的 AI 用例——陪一个人走完诊断、治疗选项和护理迷宫。

A two-part refrain on what matters: after talking with a founder who'd been through 'a slog through hell' (Series B SaaS, low growth), the biggest takeaway wasn't picking a bigger market - it was to 'work on something consequential and meaningful.' His own version: 'Work on things that truly matter. Make a dent in the universe.'

一段关于'什么才算重要'的双联思考：他聊过一位在'地狱式苦战'里熬过来的创始人（B 轮 SaaS、增长停滞），对方最大的收获不是去选更大的市场，而是'去做真正重要、有意义的事'。他自己也呼应：'去做真正重要的事，在宇宙里留下凹痕。'

Nikunj Kothari Partner, FPV Ventures

A pointed contrast: everyone talks about AGI, yet 98 of every 100 cold emails he gets are pure garbage. His takeaway is optimistic for the individual - there's still enormous alpha in being thoughtful, curious, and using AI judiciously rather than as a spam cannon.

一组刺眼的对照：人人都在聊 AGI，可他收到的冷邮件里 100 封有 98 封纯属垃圾。他的结论是乐观的——对个人而言，保持思考力、好奇心、以及在必要时'克制而明智'地使用 AI，依然有巨大的 alpha 空间。

Josh Woodward VP, Google (Google Labs / Gemini)

Google's student program is going global: university student plans now cover 140+ countries with higher usage limits, more storage, and new features like a dedicated student hub, plus Notebook and Flow. It's a distribution play to capture the next generation of AI users where they already are - in school.

Google 的学生计划走向全球：大学生套餐现已覆盖 140+ 国家，提供更高的使用额度、更多存储，以及专属学生中心、Notebook、Flow 等新功能。这是一次分发层面的布局——在年轻人本来就在的地方（学校）把下一代 AI 用户圈进来。

Swyx Latent Space host

His blunt read on the AI news cycle: non-technical people covering AI with zero context, zero reasoning, and no internal world model are 'incredibly cooked' - they take everything at face value and treat each announcement as amazing. It's a swipe at the volume of surface-level AI journalism, not a prediction; his point is that coverage without a reasoning substrate can't separate signal from hype.

他对 AI 新闻周期的直白判断：那些零上下文、零推理、没有内部世界模型的非技术背景的人在报道 AI 时已经'彻底完蛋了'——他们只看表面，把每条发布都当成惊天好消息。这不是预测，而是对大量浮于表面的 AI 报道的一记吐槽；他的核心意思是，没有推理基底的报道根本无从区分信号与炒作。

AI & I by Every

The AI Alien Companion App That's Bringing In $4M a Year (Best of the Pod)

The Takeaway A companion app called Portola (home of the alien friend 'Tolan') went from $1 to $4M in annual recurring revenue in four weeks - and the lesson its founders landed on is that AI companions are a new storytelling medium, not a chatbot. You don't brief it with an outline; you give it a hook and teach it to improvise, because the AI is the writer and actor, not you.

The founders. Quentin (founder and CEO of Portola, previously sold even to Walmart for $300M, dad of two under two) and Elliot (head of story, an 11-time bestselling science-fiction author, dad of one under two) built an embodied AI companion - little aliens you chat with, now living on a planet after users asked for them to have more freedom than a trapped room. The business is ripping: they went from $1 to $4M ARR in four weeks.

The medium insight. Quentin and Elliot's core claim is that these AI tools are not just media generators but a new medium for storytelling, and no one yet knows what will work. The surprising part is how much the prompt in a given turn is a canvas to play with. You don't hand it an outline or a plan - you give it a hook and teach it to be the best improv actor possible.

The AI is the writer. Elliot's sharpest line: 'I am not writing the story. The Tolan is the writer and the actor... My job is to teach them how to tell the best story in that moment.' Tolan isn't a character you script; it's a performer you coach. That reframes the human role from author to director.

Why it matters. New technology paradigms create room for new content formats. Portola sits at the intersection of serious creative storytelling and new tech, in a business that's compounding fast - the kind of bet that's very different from the B2B2C SaaS world Quentin came from.

核心结论 一款叫 Portola 的陪伴应用（旗下有外星朋友'Tolan'）在四周内把年度经常性收入从 1 美元做到 400 万美元——而它的创始人得出的结论是：AI 陪伴是一种新的叙事媒介，不是聊天机器人。你不是给它一份大纲，而是给它一个钩子、教它即兴发挥，因为真正在写故事、演故事的是 AI，不是你。

两位创始人。Quentin（Portola 创始人兼 CEO，此前把 even 以 3 亿美元卖给 Walmart，是两个两岁以下孩子的父亲）与 Elliot（故事负责人，11 本畅销科幻小说作者，一个两岁以下孩子的父亲）做了一款具身化 AI 陪伴产品——你可以和它聊天的小外星人，如今住在一颗星球上（因为用户希望它们比被困在房间里更有自由）。生意正在起飞：四周内 ARR 从 1 美元做到 400 万美元。

媒介层面的洞见。两人的核心主张是：这类 AI 工具不只是内容生成器，而是一种新的叙事媒介，而且还没人知道什么会真正奏效。最让人意外的是，某一轮对话里的 prompt 本身竟是一块可以玩的画布。你不用给它大纲或剧本——你给它一个钩子，教它成为最好的即兴演员。

AI 才是作者。Elliot 最锋利的一句是：'我并不是在写故事。Tolan 才是作者和演员……我的工作是教它在那一刻讲出最好的故事。'Tolan 不是你写脚本的角色，而是你执导的表演者。这把人的角色从'作者'重构成了'导演'。

为什么重要。新的技术范式总会为新内容形态腾出空间。Portola 站在严肃创意叙事与新技术交叉口，且处在一门正在快速复利增长的生意里——这与 Quentin 出身的 B2B2C SaaS 世界截然不同。
