Aaron Levie Box CEO

A strong framing on FDEs (fast-moving deployed environments): they're real and not going away, because AI adds a non-deterministic, rapidly-changing system to workflows that were never before automated. His killer line — "if you're building an AI agent for accounting in 2026, there is no established workflow, because literally nobody has ever used one." Unlike deterministic software, agents force the customer's process to change, demand heavy customization, constant evals, and constant model updates. Great time to be an FDE.

对 FDE（前沿/快速变化的部署环境）有一句有力的判断：它们是真实存在且短期内不会消失的，因为 AI 把一套非确定性、快速变化的系统，加进了此前从未被自动化的工作流。他的金句——“如果你在 2026 年给会计做 AI agent，不存在既有的工作流，因为根本没人用过。”与传统确定性软件不同，agent 逼着客户流程改变、需要重度定制、持续跑 eval、持续纳入模型更新。做 FDE 正当其时。

The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck

The Biggest AI Deployment Nobody Talks About | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas

The Takeaway Matt Turck sits down with Sanjit Biswas, co-founder & CEO of Samsara — a ~$20B company, $2B+ ARR, profitable and growing ~30%, running what may be the largest AI deployment in the physical world: millions of vehicles, 25 trillion data points a year, driving 99% of US roads daily. The load-bearing argument: the AI boom is really an infrastructure construction project, and the moat isn't the model — it's the operational, physical-world data you can't crawl online.

What physical AI is. Biswas frames physical AI as applying AI to the physical world — construction sites, the electrical grid, "all the plumbing under the street." The hard part is that it isn't digitized; unlike the digital world (petabytes of tokens that bootstrapped the models), the physical world is messy and carries a hardware component ("hardware is hard"). That means edge inference — weights pushed down from the cloud, running at many frames per second at the edge for low-latency detection and closed-loop alerting to the driver.

The moat is the data you can't crawl. On whether models commoditize: Samsara's wedge is the operational, physical-world digitization layer. "These are not the tokens you're gonna find online. You can't crawl Reddit and find out about what happened on a construction site." And there's a data network effect — because every customer drives the same roads, Samsara's system covers 99% of US roads daily, so risk learnings transfer across customers and geographies.

Agents in operations. The jump from single-turn chatbot Q&A to agents that operate over long time horizons. Demo: a warranty agent that sees a fault code, cracks the service manual, checks your negotiated OEM warranty terms, confirms coverage, opens a work order with steps, and asks whether other trucks have the same issue — collapsing 1–2 hours of human labor to under a minute. AI also reasons over video, telling a distracted driver apart from someone swerving to avoid a deer.

Impact and the human side. Samsara estimates it helped prevent ~380,000 crashes last year and avoided billions of pounds of CO2 through route optimization and reduced idling. Biswas stresses a positive-reinforcement culture — showcasing defensive driving and "exonerations" — so the tech reads as support, not surveillance. On humanoids and autonomous trucks: robots already cut lifting injuries in warehouses, and he expects the field to follow, with humans still owning the nuanced handoffs.

Scale and beginner's mind. 25 trillion data points a year. As a second-time founder (Meraki → Samsara), he credits a "reset" — selling direct to operations buyers rather than technical IT buyers — with forcing the beginner's mind he thinks most companies need.

核心结论 Matt Turck 对话 Samsara 联合创始人兼 CEO Sanjit Biswas——一家约 200 亿美元市值、ARR 超 20 亿、盈利且以约 30% 增长的公司，运营着可能是物理世界里最大规模的 AI 部署：数百万辆车、每年 25 万亿个数据点、每天跑遍美国 99% 的道路。最承重的一条论点是：这轮 AI 热潮本质上是个基础设施建设工程，而护城河不在模型——在于那些你没法从网上爬到的、运营层面的物理世界数据。

什么是物理 AI。Biswas 把物理 AI 定义为把 AI 应用到物理世界——工地、电网、“街底下所有的管道”。难点在于它还没有被数字化；和数字世界（用 PB 级 token 把模型 bootstrap 起来）不同，物理世界很乱，还有硬件成分（“硬件很难”）。这意味着需要边缘推理——权重从云下推，在边缘以多帧每秒运行，做低延迟检测和给司机的闭环告警。

护城河是你爬不到的数据。在被问到模型是否商品化时，Samsara 的切入点是运营层、物理世界的数字化。“这些不是你能在网上找到的 token。你没法爬 Reddit 去知道工地上发生了什么。”而且存在数据网络效应：因为所有客户都跑在同一条路上，Samsara 的系统每天覆盖美国 99% 的道路，所以风险方面的经验可以在客户与地域间迁移。

运营里的 agent。从单轮问答式聊天机器人，转向在长时间跨度上自主运行的 agent。演示：一个保修 agent，看到故障码就翻开维修手册、核对你与 OEM 谈的保修条款、确认是否在保、开一张带步骤的工单，还顺带问“其他车有没有同样问题”——把 1–2 小时的人工压到 1 分钟以内。AI 还能对视频推理：区分“走神的司机”和“在避让鹿的司机”。

影响与人性的一面。Samsara 估算去年帮避免了约 38 万起事故，并通过路线优化、减少怠速省下数十亿磅 CO2。Biswas 强调“正向强化”的文化——展示防御性驾驶和“免责/澄清”案例——让技术读起来是支持而非监控。关于人形机器人/自动驾驶卡车：仓库里机器人已经减少了搬运类工伤，他预期现场也会类似，但那些需要细腻交接的活仍由人做。

规模与“初心”。每年 25 万亿数据点。作为二度创业的人（Meraki → Samsara），他把一次“重置”——直接服务运营买家而非技术型 IT 买家——归功于逼出了初心，而他认为多数公司都需要这种初心。
