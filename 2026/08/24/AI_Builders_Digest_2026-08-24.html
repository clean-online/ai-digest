Today's Signal The day's builders converge on a single theme — the bottleneck has moved from raw capability to the plumbing of trust, evaluation, and adoption. Meanwhile Zara and Nikunj remind us the real constraints are human — org friction and the rising tide of slop.

Madhu Guru Sr Director, AI at Meta

On evals, part 6: hill climbing on evals is just picking a dimension that matters and optimizing for it — improving quality on high-value journeys, expanding to adjacent use cases, lowering cost or latency. The real work is better harnesses and model selection via prompt eng, context eng, memory, post-training, even deterministic code. Your failure-mode taxonomy (from part 3) is the compass for where the product struggles.

关于 evals，第 6 篇：在评测上“爬山”不过是挑一个重要的维度去优化——把高价值用户路径的质量做上去、扩展到相邻用例、降低成本和延迟。真正的工作落在更好的 harness 和模型选择上，手段包括 prompt 工程、上下文工程、记忆、后训练，甚至老派‘确定性代码’。你从（第 3 篇）建立的“失败模式分类法”，就是产品在哪里挣扎的指南针。

Aaron Levie Box CEO

AI diffusion is far more rate-limited by having good evals than most realize. The evals you see at every model release are helpful but only describe general AI progress and relative capability. The far bigger space is evals on every enterprise workflow, down to a single company's specifics — and you can't automate what you can't assess. Enterprises won't run on vibes.

AI 的扩散，比大多数人意识到的更受“有没有好 eval”的限制。每次模型发布时看到的那些 eval 很有用，但只描述通用 AI 进展和模型相对能力。更大的空间，是针对每一个企业工作流、甚至细化到某一家公司具体情境的 eval——而你无法自动化一个你无法评估进展的东西。企业不会靠“感觉”运转。

Zara Zhang Builder / Harvard '17

A pattern she's watching: a talented person can hit 10x their potential thanks to AI when working on their own thing — but inside a large org that same person is at best +20% (sometimes negative). That's why she sees more talented people leaving big companies (except maybe the top AI labs).

她观察到的一个现象：一个有能力的人，在单干时借助 AI 可以把潜力放大到 10 倍——但放进大组织里，这个人最多只提升 20%（有时甚至是负的）。这就是为什么她看到越来越多有才华的人离开大公司（大概只有顶尖 AI lab 例外）。

Microsoft’s Vision for an Internet Made for Agents With CTO Kevin Scott (Best of the Pod)

The Takeaway The AI & I team revisits a "Best of the Pod" episode with Microsoft CTO Kevin Scott on building an internet made for agents. The thesis: as agents become primary consumers of the web, the stack needs to shift from documents-for-humans to machine-readable interfaces, structured actions, and protocols that let agents negotiate, transact, and verify on behalf of users.

The big idea.The AI & I team revisits a "Best of the Pod" episode with Microsoft CTO Kevin Scott on building an internet made for agents. The thesis: as agents become primary consumers of the web, the stack needs to shift from documents-for-humans to machine-readable interfaces, structured actions, and protocols that let agents negotiate, transact, and verify on behalf of users.

Why it matters.Scott frames this as the next platform shift — an agentic web where the unit of interaction is a task, not a pageview. It reframes every layer (identity, payments, search, content) around software that acts, not just renders.

The constraint.The hard part isn't the models — it's the connective tissue: standards, trust, and the economics of who pays for an agent's actions. Without that plumbing, an agent-native internet stays a demo.

核心结论 AI & I 团队重播了一期“最佳精选”，请来微软 CTO Kevin Scott，谈如何为 agent 打造一张“属于 agent 的互联网”。核心论点：当 agent 成为网络的主要消费者，整个技术栈要从“给人看的文档”转向机器可读的接口、结构化动作，以及能让 agent 代表用户谈判、交易、验证的协议。

核心构想。AI & I 团队重播了一期“最佳精选”，请来微软 CTO Kevin Scott，谈如何为 agent 打造一张“属于 agent 的互联网”。核心论点：当 agent 成为网络的主要消费者，整个技术栈要从“给人看的文档”转向机器可读的接口、结构化动作，以及能让 agent 代表用户谈判、交易、验证的协议。

为何重要。Scott 把它定义为下一次平台级迁移——一个以“任务”而非“页面浏览”为交互单元的人机共生网络。它让身份、支付、搜索、内容每一层都围绕“会行动的软件”而非“只会渲染的软件”重新设计。

真正的约束。难点不在模型，而在连接层：标准、信任，以及“谁为 agent 的动作买单”的经济逻辑。没有这套底层工程，agent 原生的互联网就只是一个 demo。
