Today's Signal The day's builders converge on a quiet thesis: the moat isn't the model, it's the surrounding software and human judgment. Rauch wants software that gets smaller and prompt-able; Steinberger wants software you can change with a prompt; Levie says systems of record matter more once agents do 100X the work; Madhu warns that a blunt eval score hides where models actually fail; Amjad's Replit Agent quietly replaced CoWork in his own workflow; and Garry distills founder success down to "form a view, test it against reality, revise."

Madhu Guru Sr Director, AI at Meta

On evals, part 8: the discriminatory property. A hill-climbing eval only earns its keep when it can separate AI systems that are meaningfully different. His example — five systems scoring 94/93/95/94/92 — shows that if you already know A and C are substantially different but the eval can't tell them apart, the eval isn't doing its job.

关于 evals 第 8 篇：区分性。一个用于爬坡优化的评测，只有当它能区分“真正不同的 AI 系统”时才值钱。他举了五个系统分别得 94/93/95/94/92 的例子——如果你早就知道 A 和 C 有本质差别，而评测却分不出来，那这个评测就没起作用。

Guillermo Rauch Vercel CEO

A thesis on software entropy: "It's faster, cheaper, more capable, and… smaller." As software evolves it tends to get slower, more bloated, buggier, and bigger — so he architects products from the outset to prevent that creep.

一段关于软件熵的论断：“它更快、更便宜、更强，而且……更小。”软件在演化中往往会变得更慢、更臃肿、更多 bug、更庞大——所以他从一开始就把产品架构成能阻止这种膨胀。

https://x.com/rauchg/status/2092081554814320677
Guillermo Rauch Vercel CEO

A classic Rauch Zig anecdote: his terminal reset was oddly slow, traced to a sleep(1) that 3BSD's tset added in 1979 to let mechanical printer-and-ink terminals settle. He asked fx to write a faster alternative in Zig — it now takes 1ms instead of 1s.

一段典型的 Rauch 式 Zig 小花絮：他的终端 reset 慢得离谱，追因到 1979 年 3BSD 的 tset 为了等机械针式终端“稳定”而加的 sleep(1)。他让 fx 用 Zig 写个更快的替代版——现在从 1 秒变成 1 毫秒。

Aaron Levie Box CEO

Systems of record have never been more important: in a world where AI agents do 100X more work on these platforms than people ever did, agents will be querying the data, processing tasks, executing workflows, and collaborating with human and agent users.

记录系统（systems of record）从没像现在这么重要：在一个 AI agent 在这些平台上做的活比人多出 100 倍的 world 里，agent 会查询数据、处理任务、执行流程，并与人和其它 agent 协作。

Aaron Levie Box CEO

He notes ZDR (zero data retention) is underappreciated as a driver of AI adoption — it dramatically simplified the compliance process companies face when handling data with subprocessors. Without it, adoption would have been far slower.

他指出 ZDR（零数据保留）对 AI 普及的拉动被严重低估——它大幅简化了企业在使用子处理方处理数据时的合规流程。没有它，采纳速度会慢得多。
