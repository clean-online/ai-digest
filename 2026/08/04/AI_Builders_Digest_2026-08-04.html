Aaron Levie Box CEO

Argued the counterintuitive point that some of the “hardest” work in the world — math, cyber, code — gets automated first precisely because it’s verifiable: training gets clearer reward signals and runtime results can be tested at scale. Domains like legal, marketing, sales and finance lack instant verifiability, so the applied AI layer (and even changed processes) will matter more than the model itself.

提出了一个反直觉的观点：世界上某些“最难”的工作——数学、安全、编程——反而会最先被自动化，正是因为“可验证”。训练能得到更清晰的奖励信号，运行结果也能大规模测试。而法律、营销、销售、财务这类领域缺乏即时可验证性，所以真正拉开差距的会是应用层 AI（甚至包括被改造的流程），而不只是模型本身。

Dan Shipper Every CEO

Laid out a three-stage “agency rupture” model for how people metabolize AI doing tasks they used to own: (1) initial rupture — you only see the AI; (2) seeing human scaffolding — you notice the work around the AI; (3) agency reconstruction — the model becomes invisible and you re-center yourself. The ability to metabolize ruptures into playfulness is, he argues, a leading indicator for thriving in the AI economy. Also: if “ought implies can” and technology reshapes human ability, then technology reshapes our moral oughts.

提出了一个三阶段的“能动性断裂（agency rupture）”模型，描述人们如何消化“AI 做了自己过去的工作”这件事：(1) 初始断裂——你只看见 AI；(2) 看见人的脚手架——你注意到 AI 周围那些人的工作；(3) 能动性重建——模型变得不可见，你重新把自己放回中心。他认为，能把断裂转化成玩心和好奇的能力，是在 AI 经济中如鱼得水的领先指标。另一条：如果“应然蕴含能然（ought implies can）”，而技术重塑了人的能力，那么技术也就在重塑我们的道德“应然”。

Building the Automated AGI Lab: Core Automation’s Jerry Tworek and Rohan Anil

The Takeaway Jerry Tworek and Rohan Anil — the ex-OpenAI reasoning lead and ex-Gemini pretraining lead behind Core Automation — argue the transformer has quietly hit its ceiling. The real edge now is a new architecture plus end-to-end pretraining/RL plus kernel generation, pursued inside the most automated lab they can build.

Jerry Tworek (former VP at OpenAI, ran the strawberry and reasoning teams) and Rohan Anil (one of four Gemini pretraining leads) founded Core Automation, one of the hottest neo-labs in San Francisco. Their contrarian premise: the architecture is now the bottleneck, not scale.

Tworek’s spicy take — “the first step to replacing transformers is appreciating deeply how far they carried us” — means don’t re-solve what they do well; attack their weaknesses. He admits he was an RL maximalist who thought scaling reinforcement learning would “solve everything.” Benchmarks climbed, but real-world tasks didn’t, because training data never replicated the messy real-world distribution. His conclusion: we need models that learn at test time, on users’ own data.

Anil’s angle is efficiency. The transformer’s “computational depth is poor” — at most ~100 layers — and chain-of-thought is a band-aid that adds depth one token at a time, which is wildly inefficient at inference. He wants architectures that spend computation much better than autoregressive token generation.

Both believe pretraining and RL should be optimized end-to-end, something most labs don’t do because of org charts and release-cycle competition (“tokens aren’t sticky”). Their six-month roadmap centers on kernel generation: a QR linear-algebra kernel they got 60x faster only with a scarce human expert plus coding agents — models today can’t touch it. That kernel loop is their inner path to more efficient architectures.

On AGI, Tworek defines it as a model that improves itself with no human in the loop; the current path doesn’t get there because transformers can’t do continual learning. The bet: build the most automated lab and experiment your way to a superior architecture — ideally one where the team can go on vacation and the lab still ships something better.

Jerry Tworek（前 OpenAI VP，曾负责 strawberry 和推理团队）与 Rohan Anil（Gemini 四位预训练负责人之一）创办了 Core Automation，是旧金山最炙手可热的 neo-lab 之一。他们反共识的前提是：瓶颈已经不是规模，而是架构本身。

Tworek 那条惹眼的观点——“替代 transformer 的第一步，是深深感激它把我们带到了多远”——意思是别去重新解决它已经解决得很好的问题，要去打它的弱点。他坦承自己曾是 RL maximalist，以为把强化学习规模化就能“解决一切”。基准分数涨了，真实世界的任务却没解决，因为训练数据从未复刻真实世界那团混乱的分布。他的结论：我们需要能在测试时、在用户自己的数据上持续学习的模型。

Anil 的切入点是效率。transformer 的“计算深度很浅”——最多大约 100 层——而思维链（chain-of-thought）是一块创可贴，一次只加一个 token 的深度，在推理时极其低效。他要的是比自回归逐 token 生成更会“花计算”的架构。

两人都相信预训练和 RL 应该端到端一起优化，而大多数实验室不这么做，原因是组织架构和发布节奏的竞争（“token 不黏人”）。他们六个月的路线图以 kernel 生成为核心：一个 QR 线性代数 kernel，只有稀缺的人类专家加上 coding agent 才做到了 60 倍加速——今天的模型根本碰不了。这个 kernel 循环，正是他们通往更高效架构的内部路径。

谈到 AGI，Tworek 把它定义为“无需人类在环就能自我改进”的模型；当前路径到不了，因为 transformer 做不了持续学习。他们的赌注是：建一个最自动化的实验室，用实验“试”出更优架构——理想情况下，团队去度假，实验室还在产出更好的东西。
