Guillermo Rauch Vercel CEO

Guillermo Rauch (Vercel CEO) pushes his “factory over prompts” thesis: when you have a new idea, don’t just ad-hoc prompt an agent — build the factory that will start, maintain and grow it. “The (software) factory is the product”; your product is only as good as the agents you set up to autonomously maintain it, the same lesson Musk drew from Tesla. He also details a filesystem-native research workflow: a research/ folder + an AGENTS.md describing his preferred format, then launch an agent to find and correlate knowledge across sessions — no fancy apps or knowledge graphs, synced via iCloud/git, rendered to HTML reports on demand.

Guillermo Rauch（Vercel CEO）力推「工厂优先于提示词」的观点：有了新想法，不要临时去给 agent 写 prompt，而要搭建那个能启动、维护并growing它的工厂。「（软件）工厂本身就是产品」；你的产品只会和你设好、能自主维护它的 agent 一样好——这正是 Musk 从特斯拉悟到的道理。他还拆解了一套基于文件系统的研究工作流：一个 research/ 文件夹 + 一份写清自己偏好格式的 AGENTS.md，然后启动 agent 跨会话检索并关联知识——不用花哨的 app 或知识图谱，靠 iCloud/git 同步，需要时渲染成 HTML 报告。

Unsupervised Learning

Ep 91: Benedict Evans on Today’s AI Hype Cycle — Where Value Accrues & Why Consumer AI Isn’t Working

The Takeaway Analyst Benedict Evans gives a deliberately “subdued” take on the AI hype cycle: model capability isn’t the bottleneck — the ability to isolate and describe the tasks worth automating is. Most people aren’t tool builders, don’t see the problem a tool would solve, and couldn’t build it anyway. That’s why enterprise AI is stuck at one-pain-point-at-a-time pilots, and why “enterprise transformation” (and consulting) will do the heavy lifting.

Unsupervised Learning’s Jacob Efron sits down with analyst Benedict Evans for a deliberately measured read on the AI hype cycle. Evans’ core claim: the constraint isn’t model capability — it’s your ability to isolate and describe the things you could hand to the model. Using himself as an example (a solo operator with no interns and few repetitive tasks), he notes that having Claude, Copilot and ChatGPT doesn’t help if you don’t actually have the tasks to give them. That gap — seeing you have the problem, and realizing you could give it to the system — is hardest for voice and chat interfaces.

With every new technology, he argues, we first do the old thing faster, forcing the new tool into old workflows; the genuinely new use cases take much longer to imagine and usually aren’t automation of existing work at all. The deeper reason enterprise adoption is slow: most people are three things at once — not tool builders, unable to see the problem a tool would solve, and not in a position to build it even if they could. The skill of being a great salesperson is completely different from designing great sales-enablement software.

On creativity, Evans draws a sharp line: AI can generate more variance — more “bad jazz,” more Taylor-Swift-like pop — but inventing punk or hip-hop is a different thing entirely (though he concedes 99% of enterprise work isn’t inventing hip-hop). He also flags a recurring pattern: outsiders imagine one flashy use for AI in an industry, while insiders say the real value is automating the boring back-office work — the “faxes” of advertising, the customer research in consulting that now takes a day instead of a week.

His framing of enterprise adoption: step one is buying seats (“we bought everyone Claude”), which mostly doesn’t make sense; step two is pilots, of which about half get deployed and work fine — but one at a time. Then the real question surfaces: is that it? Automating one pain point isn’t structural change. Reimagining how an organization works around a new technology is a big, difficult project — what used to be called digital transformation and is now “enterprise transformation.” That, Evans notes wryly, is exactly why professional services exist.

Unsupervised Learning 的 Jacob Efron 与分析师 Benedict Evans 对谈，对 AI 炒作周期给出一个刻意克制的判断。Evans 的核心观点：瓶颈不是模型能力——而是你把可交给模型的任务识别并描述清楚的能力。他以自己为例（一个没有实习生、也几乎没有重复性任务的单人操作者）：就算有了 Claude、Copilot 和 ChatGPT，如果你根本没有任务可以派给它们，也没用。这道鸿沟——意识到你有这个问题、并意识到你可以把它交给系统——对语音和聊天界面来说最难跨越。

他认为，每一种新技术出现时，我们都先是把旧事做得更快，硬把新工具塞进旧流程；真正全新的用例要花长得多的时间才能想到，而且通常根本不是对现有工作的自动化。企业采用之所以慢，更深的原因是：大多数人同时是三种人——不是工具构建者、看不到某个工具能解决的问题、以及即便看到了也没有能力去造它。当一名出色销售的技能，和设计一款出色销售赋能软件的技能，完全是两回事。

谈到创造力，Evans 划出一条清晰的界线：AI 能生成更多「方差」——更多「难听的爵士」、更多类似 Taylor Swift 的流行乐——但发明朋克或嘻哈完全是另一回事（不过他也承认，企业里 99% 的工作并不是发明嘻哈）。他还点出一个反复出现的模式：外行会想象 AI 在某行业里某个炫酷用途，而内行会说真正的价值在于自动化那些无聊的后台工作——广告业的「发传真」、咨询业里如今一天就能做完、过去要一周的客户调研。

他对企业采用的刻画是：第一步是买席位（「我们给每个人都买了 Claude」），而这在多数情况下并不成立；第二步是 pilot，其中约一半会被部署、也确实能用——但都是一次一个。接着真正的问题浮现：就这？自动化一个痛点并不是结构性变革。围绕一项新技术重新想象一个组织如何运转，是一个庞大而困难的项目——过去叫数字化转型，现在叫「企业转型」。Evans 揶揄道，这恰恰就是专业服务存在的理由。

An update on recent Claude Code quality reports (April 23 postmortem)

Anthropic published a postmortem on reports that Claude’s responses had worsened for some users over the past month. They traced the reports to three separate changes affecting Claude Code, the Claude Agent SDK, and Claude Cowork — the API itself was not impacted. All three issues were resolved as of April 20 (v2.1.116).

The post stresses a principle worth repeating: Anthropic states it never intentionally degrades its models, and it walks through what was found, what was fixed, and what it will do differently to make similar regressions much less likely — a transparency move aimed at rebuilding trust with heavy Claude Code users.

Anthropic 发布了一份复盘，针对过去一个月里「部分用户反馈 Claude 回复质量变差」的问题。他们把这些反馈追溯到三处独立的改动，分别影响 Claude Code、Claude Agent SDK 和 Claude Cowork——API 本身未受影响。三个问题已于 4 月 20 日（v2.1.116）全部修复。

文章强调了一条值得反复重申的原则：Anthropic 表示自己从不刻意削弱模型，并逐一说明了发现了什么、修了什么、以及未来会怎样改变做法以大幅降低类似回退再次发生的概率——这是一次意在向重度 Claude Code 用户重建信任的透明化举措。

Anthropic Engineering

Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands

Anthropic’s Engineering blog continues its running theme on building effective agents and designing harnesses for long-running work. The key insight: harnesses encode assumptions about what Claude can’t do on its own — and those assumptions go stale as models improve, so they must be questioned frequently.

One concrete example: earlier work found Claude Sonnet 4.5 would wrap up tasks prematurely as it sensed its context limit approaching — a behavior dubbed “context anxiety.” The Managed Agents architecture leans into decoupling the brain (reasoning) from the hands (execution), letting the model focus on decisions while the harness handles orchestration at scale.

Anthropic 工程博客延续其长期主题——如何构建有效的 agent、以及为长时任务设计 harness（挽具）。核心洞见是：harness 里编码了「Claude 自己做不到什么」的假设——而随着模型变强，这些假设会过期，因此必须被频繁质疑。

一个具体例子：此前的研究发现 Claude Sonnet 4.5 会在感到上下文即将触顶时过早收尾任务——一种被称为「上下文焦虑」的行为。Managed Agents 架构着重把「大脑」（推理）和「手」（执行）解耦，让模型专注于决策，而由 harness 在规模化层面负责编排。

Claude Blog

New in Claude Managed Agents: self-hosted sandboxes and MCP tunnels

Claude Managed Agents can now operate in a sandbox you control and connect to your private Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. Both the sandbox where an agent executes tools and the services it reaches run within your enterprise’s established boundaries — under your own security and runtime controls.

The sandbox runs on your own infrastructure, or with managed providers like Cloudflare, Daytona, Modal or Vercel handling compute and isolation. On the Claude Platform, self-hosted sandboxes are in public beta and MCP tunnels are in research preview — a clear push toward enterprise-grade, security-contained agent deployment.

Claude Managed Agents 现在可以在你自己掌控的沙箱中运行，并连接到你的私有 Model Context Protocol（MCP）服务器。无论是 agent 执行工具的沙箱，还是它访问的服务，都运行在你企业既定的边界之内——受你自己的安全与运行时控制。

沙箱可以跑在你自己的基础设施上，也可以交给 Cloudflare、Daytona、Modal 或 Vercel 这类托管方来处理算力与隔离。在 Claude 平台上，自托管沙箱已进入公测，MCP 隧道处于研究预览——明确指向企业级、安全隔离的 agent 部署方向。
