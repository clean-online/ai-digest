The Takeaway

Today's feed is one launch with four Anthropic accounts posting it, and the launch is not a feature — it is a change in what the user is asked to hold. Boris Cherny's line is the plainest statement of it: "I stopped managing sessions. I just send thoughts as they come, Claude splits them into threads, and the project remembers how I work." Cat Wu supplies the abstraction above it — "I fire off a batch of tasks and move on. Claude has context on everything I'm working on" — and Thariq supplies the shape underneath: "one agent per project that manages memory and spins off subagents for tasks." Yesterday's news was Anthropic collapsing chat and Cowork into one surface; today's is the unit of organisation moving from the session to the project.

The boundary is stated as honestly as the capability. Threads "run in the cloud, so they keep working even when your computer is offline," but they "can't reach files or tools on your computer or your internal network yet. Local support is coming very soon." So the project is continuous and the machine is not, and that gap is where the current limits are.

Read against Aaron Levie, the same move is happening in the market rather than the product. He says agents "already make up the majority of inference," will trend "toward nearly all inference over the next year or two," and that "the vast majority of tokens used in the world will be agents that are executing unbelievable amounts of tasks for us in the background 24/7." His pace claim is the one worth keeping, because it is dated and falsifiable: "Just in the past week I've introduced multiple completely new workflows that would not have been possible technically even a month ago." A project that wakes up on a schedule is the product form of that sentence.

The evidence for it today is small and real rather than large and projected. Nikunj Kothari's nosugarforkids runs a Claude agent that "wakes up once a day" to check the catalog, prune dead products, find content ideas, read Search Console data, write and edit pieces, and now "reaches out to publications to get backlinks" — and after no distribution at all it is at "~6k impressions a day and 60 clicks." Rauch's numbers sit on the supply side of the same story: 1 billion deployments in ten years, "another 1.4 billion in the last 10 months alone," with deploy time down to 1 second, because "more software will be produced next year than in all of the history of computing."

Two smaller threads. Google is pushing the same coordination idea at a household instead of a developer — CC for families, up to 5 members, and one line on scope that generalises: it "remembers what applies to everyone ... versus what applies to one person." And the safety argument is being funded outside the labs: Aditya Agarwal calls GoodfireAI "the leading non-frontier-lab company that is working on this" and the work "generationally important," while Garry Tan restates the goal as "alignment to humankind vs any other goal."

核心结论

今天的 feed 是一次发布、四个 Anthropic 账号在发，而这次发布不是一个功能，是"用户需要自己拿着的东西"变了。Boris Cherny 那句说得最直白："我不再管理会话了。我脑子里冒出什么就发什么，Claude 把它们拆成线程，而项目记得我的工作习惯。"Cat Wu 补上了它上面那一层抽象——"我扔出去一批任务就去干别的了。Claude 对我手上所有事情都有上下文"——Thariq 补上了下面的结构："每个项目有一个 agent，负责管理记忆，并为各项任务派生子 agent"。昨天的新闻是 Anthropic 把 chat 和 Cowork 收敛成一个界面；今天的则是组织单位从"会话"移到了"项目"。

边界和能力被讲得一样诚实。线程"跑在云端，所以即使你的电脑离线它们也继续工作"，但它们"暂时还无法触达你电脑上的文件或工具、以及你的内网。本地支持很快就来"。所以项目是连续的，机器不是——当前的所有限制都在这个缺口里。

和 Aaron Levie 那条放在一起读，同样的迁移正发生在市场上，而不是产品里。他说 agent"已经占了推理的大头"，会在"未来一两年内"走向"接近全部推理"，而且"世界上消耗的绝大多数 token，都将是那些在后台 7×24 为我们执行海量任务的 agent"。其中最值得留下来的是他那句关于速度的话，因为它标了日期、也可以被证伪："光是过去这一周，我就引入了多个全新的工作流，而它们在一个月前技术上根本做不到。"一个能按日程自己醒过来的项目，就是这句话在产品形态上的样子。

今天的证据是小而实的，不是大而预测的。Nikunj Kothari 的 nosugarforkids 跑着一个每天"醒来一次"的 Claude agent：检查目录、清掉失效商品、找内容选题、读 Search Console 数据、撰写和编辑内容，现在还会"主动联系媒体换外链"——在完全没有分发的情况下，它做到了"每天约 6,000 次曝光和 60 次点击"。Rauch 的数字落在同一个故事的供给侧：十年做到 10 亿次部署，"光是过去 10 个月就又加了 14 亿次"，部署时间压到 1 秒，因为"明年生产出来的软件，很可能比整个计算史上加起来的还要多"。

另有两条较小的线。Google 在把同一个"协调"的想法推向家庭而不是开发者——面向家庭的 CC，最多 5 名成员，其中有一句关于记忆范围的话具有普适性：它"会区分哪些是适用于所有人的……哪些只适用于某一个人"。而安全这条线正在实验室之外获得资金：Aditya Agarwal 称 GoodfireAI 是"做这件事的公司里、非前沿实验室中最领先的一家"，并说这项工作"具有代际意义"；Garry Tan 则把目标重述为"对齐于人类，而不是任何其他目标"。
