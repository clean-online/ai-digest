Aaron Levie CEO, Box

Box CEO Aaron Levie posts the day's structural argument, and it is the same one he makes at length on today's podcast: the gap is not in the model. "There's a massive chasm between the power of AI models and the ultimate workflows that enterprises are trying to automate. This gap is the opportunity for the applied AI layer to fill." His list of what fills it is operational rather than technical — "connect the intelligence to workflows, often reengineer processes, aggregate the right context and data, allow for the right human in the loop experiences, drive change management, do domain specific evals, manage the security and governance of the data and process." The counterintuitive part is his claim about what better models do to this layer: "even as models improve at incredible rates, this layer still must exist - and may become even more important and useful. Greater capability enables even more complex tasks to be tackled, amplifying the challenges if you don't do this well."

Box CEO Aaron Levie 给出了今天的结构性论点，和他今天在播客里展开讲的是同一件事：缺口不在模型。"AI 模型的能力，与企业最终想要自动化的工作流之间，存在一道巨大的鸿沟。填补这道鸿沟，就是应用层 AI 的机会。"他列出来用来填补这道鸿沟的东西，全是运营层面的而不是技术层面的——"把智能接到工作流上、经常要重构流程、把正确的上下文和数据聚合起来、留出正确的人类在环体验、推动变革管理、做领域专属的评测、管理数据与流程的安全和治理"。反直觉的是他对"模型变强之后这层会怎样"的判断："即便模型以难以置信的速度进步，这一层仍然必须存在，而且可能变得更重要、更有用。能力越强，能承接的任务就越复杂，而如果你这一层做不好，问题也会被放大。"

Thariq Claude Code, Anthropic

Thariq of Claude Code at Anthropic reverses a position many people hold, and gives the reasons rather than just the conclusion: "I was not expecting things to go this way, but I think MCPs are better than CLIs for most integrations." Three things changed in his telling — "The models have gotten much better at tool calling, we can defer tools & MCP is now stateless." He also pre-answers the objection about composability: "If you need to compose/filter data, add params like query to your MCP tools." It is a short post, but it is the kind of statement that gets quoted for a while because it moves the default recommendation rather than adding an option.

Anthropic Claude Code 的 Thariq 推翻了一个很多人持有的立场，而且给的是理由不是结论："我没想到会走到这一步，但我认为对大多数集成来说，MCP 比 CLI 更好。"按他的说法变了三件事——"模型在工具调用上进步很大，我们可以延迟加载工具，而且 MCP 现在是无状态的。"他还提前回答了关于可组合性的质疑："如果你需要组合或过滤数据，就给你的 MCP 工具加上 query 之类的参数。"这条很短，但属于会被引用一段时间的那种判断，因为它挪动的是默认推荐，而不是多给了你一个选项。

Dan Shipper CEO, Every

Every CEO Dan Shipper reports on roughly a week of testing a new foundation model, and the reason his team bothered is that it breaks the output assumption: "we almost never test new foundation models but we've been testing this for ~a week." What it does instead of writing is score — "it doesn't produce words as output, it produces probabilities. so it can efficiently act as a judge in cases where you'd need a Fable-level model—but in our testing was 25x faster and 600x lower priced." His forecast is deliberately boring in its time horizon: "the kind of things that will be obviously indispensible in 6-12 months." A model that is 600x cheaper at judging is the kind of number that changes what gets built on top, since evaluation stops being the expensive part.

Every CEO Dan Shipper 报告了对一个新基础模型大约一周的测试，而他们这次愿意花时间的原因是它打破了输出形式的假设："我们几乎不测新的基础模型，但这个我们测了大概一周。"它做的事不是写，而是打分——"它输出的不是词，而是概率。所以它可以高效地充当裁判，用在那些原本你需要 Fable 级别模型的场景——而在我们的测试里，它快 25 倍、便宜 600 倍。"他给的时间尺度刻意不激动人心："属于那种 6 到 12 个月后你会发现明显离不开的东西。"一个在"裁判"这件事上便宜 600 倍的模型，是会改变上层能造出什么的那类数字——因为评测不再是最贵的那部分。

Box's Aaron Levie: On Reinventing Yourself in the AI Age and Enterprise Diffusion

Aaron Levie spends most of this episode arguing that the interesting money is not in the model, and he is unusually explicit that he is arguing his own book. On whether application companies have become the hottest "neo-labs": the reason is not that models stopped mattering, it is that "there's a lot of gap between the model and the workflow," and once you build that bridge you end up with "enough data, enough sort of domain expertise where that becomes its own sort of flywheel." His historical comparison is the cloud: if you had looked at early GCP or AWS in the data space, "I guarantee you would not have predicted Snowflake or Databricks existing." His own numbers on the size of that gap: Box sits on "hundreds of billions of files," his personal Box account has "on the order of tens of millions of files," and the company runs at roughly $1.3B in revenue run rate — his point being that even that only covers a fraction of enterprise data, since "there's a lot of people not using Box" and their files are still "in on premises systems, legacy file shares."

Aaron Levie 这期大部分时间都在论证：有意思的钱不在模型里。而且他非常坦率地承认自己是在为自己的立场辩护。关于应用公司是否已经成为最热的"新实验室"，他的理由不是模型不重要了，而是"模型和实际工作流之间有很大的缝隙"，而一旦你把这道桥搭起来，就会积累出"足够的数据、足够的领域专长，让它自己变成一个飞轮"。他用的历史类比是云：如果你在数据领域看过早期的 GCP 或 AWS，"我敢保证你不会预测到 Snowflake 或 Databricks 会出现"。他给这道缝隙算的账是：Box 存着"数千亿份文件"，他个人的 Box 账户里有"大约几千万份"，公司年化收入约 13 亿美元——而他真正的意思是，即便如此也只覆盖了企业数据的一小部分，因为"还有很多人没在用 Box"，他们的文件依旧躺在"本地系统、遗留文件共享里"。

The most quotable forecast is about who starts the work: "in five years from now, I would bet, like, 90% of all tokens in the enterprise are things that a user never kicked off, and they just see a result." That is why he thinks chat is a permanent but incomplete interface — "Everybody will have it," the same way "your product has a search box," but "most of the enterprise is sort of made up of these processes and workflows that are kind of just happening behind the scenes," and those need "a dashboard... a workflow... a queue... a task list." His rule for systems of record is two-sided: build an agent that is "provably... 10 or 20 points better than an off the shelf agent at using your system," and separately "go headless" by exposing APIs to outside agents via MCP. His own evidence for the second half is behavioral — "I use Salesforce more today, probably by an order of magnitude, than I ever have because I MCP into it via Claude or ChatGPT."

最值得引用的是他关于"谁发起工作"的预测："我敢打赌，五年后企业里 90% 的 token 消耗，都是用户根本没有主动发起的，他们只看到结果。"这也是为什么他认为聊天是一个会长期存在但不完整的界面——"每家都会有"，就像"你的产品有个搜索框"一样，但"企业的大部分是由那些在后台默默运行着的流程和工作流构成的"，而这些需要的是"一个看板……一个工作流……一个队列……一个任务清单"。他给记录系统的规则是双向的：一方面要做一个"可证明地比通用 agent 在你系统里好 10 到 20 个点"的 agent；另一方面要"去掉界面"，把 API 通过 MCP 开放给外部 agent。他为后半条拿出的证据是自己的行为——"我今天用 Salesforce 的频率，大概比过去高一个数量级，因为我是通过 Claude 或 ChatGPT 的 MCP 接进去的。"

On diffusion, he explains the coding gap with six properties rather than with model quality. Code is "almost 100% represented by the amount of text that you can generate"; the labs treat it as their own benchmark and get to run their own evals daily; the audience is technical enough to fix a broken MCP server instead of calling IT; and it is "a very, very high paying vertical." Take those away and you get his sales-rep counterexample — the value is "convincing an external customer to buy," so even perfect automation is still "rate limited and constrained by... did the customer respond to them? Do they want to meet?" His conclusion for the Valley is a warning about timelines: "Silicon Valley has to prepare for diffusion taking lot longer than they think," and his conclusion for investors is that this is where the applied layer's value sits — "a trillion dollars of applied layer AI value is... how do you go get the technology to the lawyer or to the sales rep." Two smaller operational details: Box runs two evals — a "complex work eval" across life sciences, financial services, public sector and tech, plus a holdback eval on how Box employees use their own data — with "hundreds of different tests" per model, sensitive enough that "we see when things move by half a point." And on open weights, his position is nuanced: adoption is "probably higher than people think, lower than what enterprises actually want, and much, much, much, much, much lower than what it'll be in five years," with "30 plus percent" of current interest attributable to "the sexiness of... I wanna try GLM," and real remaining problems like models that "randomly... speak Chinese mid chain."

关于扩散速度，他用了六个性质来解释编码与其他领域的落差，而不是用模型质量。代码"几乎 100% 由你能生成的文本量代表"；实验室把它当作自己的基准，每天都能跑自己的评测；使用者的技术能力足以自己修好一个坏掉的 MCP 服务器，而不是打电话给 IT；而且它是"一个薪酬非常非常高的垂直领域"。把这些拿掉，就得到他举的销售反例——销售的价值在于"说服一个外部客户购买"，所以即便自动化做到完美，也仍然"被这些事限速和约束……客户回复了吗？他们愿意见面吗？"他给硅谷的结论是一条关于时间尺度的警告："硅谷必须做好准备，扩散花的时间会比他们以为的长很多"；而他给投资人的结论是，应用层价值就在这里——"一万亿美元的应用层 AI 价值，就是你怎么把技术送到律师手里、送到销售手里"。另外两个较小的运营细节：Box 跑两套评测——一套覆盖生命科学、金融服务、公共部门和科技的"复杂工作评测"，加上一套基于 Box 员工如何使用自己数据的留存评测——每个模型"几百项不同的测试"，灵敏到"模型进步半个点我们都能看到"。关于开源权重，他的立场很细腻：采用率"大概比人们以为的高，比企业真正想要的低，而比五年后会达到的水平低非常多非常多"，其中"30% 以上"的当前兴趣可以归因于"那种很酷的感觉——我想试试 GLM"，而真问题也还在，比如模型会"随机在推理中途开始说中文"。

The Takeaway

Today's feed lines up behind one claim, stated twice by the same person in two formats. On X, Aaron Levie writes that there is "a massive chasm between the power of AI models and the ultimate workflows that enterprises are trying to automate." On Training Data he spends an hour explaining why that chasm does not close as models improve — it widens, because "greater capability enables even more complex tasks to be tackled, amplifying the challenges if you don't do this well."

The rest of the feed is evidence from the layer below that argument. Thariq says MCPs now beat CLIs for most integrations, which is a claim about tool plumbing rather than intelligence. Rauch launches Vercel Labs and argues the future is multi-model and that hiding the choice "hurts customers." Dan Shipper reports a model that outputs probabilities instead of words and is "25x faster and 600x lower priced" as a judge — if evaluation gets that cheap, the applied layer's real bottleneck (Levie's "domain specific evals") gets cheaper too. Garry Tan's Capy result is the same pattern at the harness level: same frontier models, half the time.

Levies's 90% forecast is the one to hold onto, because it reframes what gets built. If most enterprise tokens are spent on work "a user never kicked off," then chat interfaces are a place to check in rather than the product, and the systems that win are the ones holding the data and the workflow — which is also why Salesforce in Claude shipping 37 pre-built skills, rather than just a connector, is the more informative announcement of the day.

核心结论

今天的 feed 都排在同一个判断后面，而这个人用两种形式把这个判断说了两遍。在 X 上，Aaron Levie 写"AI 模型的能力，与企业最终想要自动化的工作流之间存在一道巨大鸿沟"。在 Training Data 里，他花了一小时解释为什么这道鸿沟不会随着模型变强而收窄，反而会变宽——"能力越强，能承接的任务就越复杂，而如果你这一层做不好，问题也会被放大"。

feed 里剩下的内容，都是来自这个论点之下一层的证据。Thariq 说对大多数集成而言 MCP 已经胜过 CLI，这是一个关于工具管道的判断，不是关于智能的判断。Rauch 成立 Vercel Labs，主张未来是多模型的，而把这个选择藏起来"会伤害客户"。Dan Shipper 报告了一个输出概率而不是词语的模型，作为裁判"快 25 倍、便宜 600 倍"——如果评测便宜到这个程度，应用层真正的瓶颈（Levie 说的"领域专属评测"）也会跟着变便宜。Garry Tan 用 Capy 跑出的结果是同一个模式在 harness 层的体现：同样的前沿模型，一半的时间。

最值得记住的是 Levie 那个 90% 的预测，因为它重新定义了该造什么。如果企业里大部分 token 都花在"用户根本没有主动发起"的工作上，那么聊天界面就只是一个回来查看的地方，而不是产品本身；赢下来的是那些握着数据和工作流的系统——这也是为什么 Salesforce in Claude 带着 37 个预置技能、而不是只给一个连接器上线，是今天信息量更大的那条公告。
