Guillermo Rauch CEO · Vercel

One long post, and the only finished artifact in today’s feed. He ported his Mini web browser off Electron and Bun onto Rust and Swift: “It’s faster, more secure, and shockingly, nicer to iterate on than Electron + Bun.” Chromium is bundled through the cef crate; an agent is embedded via fx acp, which talks to his local fx CLI over ACP and can then drive the browser through an MCP server. The Takeaway

There is no launch, no benchmark and no new model in this feed today, so what remains is each person describing one shift in one sentence. Two of those sentences describe the same shift, written two hours apart with neither citing the other. Box CEO Aaron Levie splits the economy by where friction sits: markets that profit from customers’ friction lose it and see competition rise until some new equilibrium, while healthcare, travel, local services and certain information services are unlocked by it. Every CEO Dan Shipper compresses it into one line about an industry built on modelling humans as rational agents watching humans adopt rational agents. Neither offers a number, a customer or a market size.

The second thread is operational, and it is the closest thing to evidence today. OpenAI’s Thibault Sottiaux, who runs Codex, says code freeze is not really a thing before releases anymore and that in future the code might even be generated online per request according to constraints. Peter Steinberger, inside the same 24 hours, says he plans to let Codex decide which tests actually need to run, cut CI drastically and run tests hourly. Two people, two different codebases, the same direction: the gate in front of a release is being handed to a model. Neither published a before or after number, so this is a change of practice reported by its owner, not a measured result.

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch supplies the only finished artifact: a browser ported off Electron onto Rust and Swift, Chromium bundled through the cef crate, an agent embedded over ACP and driving the browser through an MCP server, with Liquid Glass and faster boots as the payoff. He generalises it into a market call, that the whole software world will nativify faster than people realise, and credits the port to Opus 5.5 and Sol 6. There is no benchmark in the post, and the credibility question is the one this feed has been carrying since 09-24: the person reporting the gain is the person who shipped it and picked the comparison.

The loudest counter-current is about what the gains do to us. Anthropic’s Thariq writes that he is most afraid of us eating the productivity gains of agents by becoming lazier, which is the exact opposite of what Ryo Lu argued on 09-25, that the danger is not laziness but being kept endlessly busy. Both are one sentence with nothing behind them, and both cannot be the main risk. Next to them sits Matt Turck’s observation that AI researchers tend to believe in neither doom nor uncontrollable acceleration while people without that knowledge hold the most certain views, itself unsourced, and Zara Zhang’s note that a dazzling technology can make its failures feel like your own fault.

Four things this feed has been tracking did not move today. The DevDay Sottiaux dated on 09-25 to next Tuesday falls outside this snapshot, which closes at 2026-09-28T06:57Z, so it stays open rather than counting as missed. The resets thread he closed on 09-28 with a single sentence still carries no account count, quota or window, twenty four hours later. Nobody picked up Rauch’s 09-28 line about rejecting non-understanding, and nobody reproduced Peter Yang’s Claude limit change from the same day. Nikunj Kothari’s report on Astra is today’s only capability statement, and it comes from a user on his own workload, which does not close the independent cross-model gap flagged since 09-24.

Twelve of today’s twenty five posts carry no AI content, and they are listed here rather than dropped so that the count matches what the feed carried: six periods from Sottiaux; two perfume posts from Amanda Askell; three separate posts inside one nostalgia thread from Peter Yang, Nan Yu and Thariq; a two word reaction from Amjad Masad; the Do not nerf slogan from Garry Tan, whose other two posts do carry a position on anti-bot; a book request and a device size from Nikunj Kothari; and Zara Zhang’s answer on why she posts without monetizing. The nostalgia cluster is the one worth noticing, three builders landing within four hours with no link shown between them.

核心结论

今天这份 feed 里没有发布、没有跑分、没有新模型，剩下的是每人用一句话描述同一个位移。其中两句话描述的是同一个位移，相隔两个小时写出，谁也没引谁。Box CEO Aaron Levie 按摩擦所在的位置把经济切成两半：靠用户摩擦赚钱的市场会失去它、竞争加剧直到新的均衡；医疗、旅行、本地服务和部分信息服务则被它打开。Every CEO Dan Shipper 把它压成一句：一个建立在 “把人建模为理性 agent” 之上的行业，正在看着人类开始采用理性 agent。两人都没给出数字、客户或市场规模。

第二条线在操作层，也是今天最接近证据的东西。OpenAI 负责 Codex 的 Thibault Sottiaux 说发布前的 code freeze 已经不再真是个东西，未来代码甚至可能按每次请求的约束在线生成。Peter Steinberger 在同一天内说他打算让 Codex 决定哪些测试真的需要跑，大幅砍掉 CI，改为每小时跑一次。两个人、两个代码库、同一个方向：发布前那道闸门正在交给模型。两人都没公布改动前后的数字，所以这是由实践者自己报告的改动，不是一个被度量的结果。

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 给出了唯一的成品：一个从 Electron 移植到 Rust 与 Swift 的浏览器，Chromium 通过 cef crate 打包，agent 经 ACP 嵌入、由 MCP server 驱动浏览器，换来的是 Liquid Glass 与更快的启动。他把它推成市场判断，整个软件世界 nativify 的速度会比人们意识到的更快，并把这次移植归功于 Opus 5.5 与 Sol 6。帖子里没有跑分，可信度问题也正是这个 feed 从 09-24 起一直在背的那个：报告收益的人，就是发布它、并选定了比较对象的人。

最响的反向声音是关于这些收益会怎么对待我们。Anthropic 的 Thariq 说他最怕的是我们用变懒把 agent 的生产力收益吃掉，这与 Ryo Lu 在 09-25 那期的判断正好相反，后者说危险不是变懒，而是被弄得无止境地忙碌。两句都是孤零零一句话、背后什么都没有，而它们不可能同时是主要风险。挨着它们的是 Matt Turck 的观察：AI 研究者往往既不信末日也不信不可控加速，而缺乏这层知识的人反而持有最确定的看法（这条本身也没有出处）；以及 Zara Zhang 那句，炫目的技术会让它失效的部分看起来像你自己的错。

这个 feed 在追的四件事今天没有动。Sottiaux 在 09-25 定在 “下周二” 的 DevDay 落在本次快照窗口之外（窗口截止 2026-09-28T06:57Z），所以它仍然是开着的，不能算作失约。他在 09-28 用一句话收掉的 resets 线，过了 24 小时仍然没有账户数、额度或时间窗。Rauch 09-28 那句关于拒绝不理解的帖子没有人接话，Peter Yang 同一天的 Claude 限额变化也没有第二个人复现。Nikunj Kothari 对 Astra 的描述是今天唯一的能力陈述，出自使用者自己的负载，补不上这个 feed 从 09-24 起就标记出的独立跨模型缺口。

今天 25 条里 12 条没有 AI 内容，这里逐条列出而不是删掉，好让数字与 feed 承载的一致：Sottiaux 的六个句号；Amanda Askell 的两条香水帖；Peter Yang、Nan Yu、Thariq 落在同一串怀旧话题里的三条；Amjad Masad 两个词的反应；Garry Tan 的 Do not nerf 口号（他另外两条对反爬是有立场的）；Nikunj Kothari 的求书与机型尺寸；以及 Zara Zhang 回答为什么不靠内容变现。值得留意的是那串怀旧，三位 builder 在四小时内先后发出，彼此之间看不出关联。
