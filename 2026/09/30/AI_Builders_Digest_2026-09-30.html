Aaron Levie CEO & Box

One post, and it is the only entry today that comes with a named evaluation and a stated baseline. Box tested Sonnet 5.5 in early access on its own “complex work eval” with the Box Agent, against Sonnet 5. The headline is +4 points overall on their hardest tests, roughly 2.4× faster to a finished deliverable on 12% fewer tokens. By vertical: +18 percentage points in Financial Services, +7 in Legal, +8 in Life Sciences, +7 in the Public Sector. He then lists four task-level wins that are far larger than the aggregate. Financial services (+48 points): a due-diligence review of a trading-tool acquisition where the deal book’s arithmetic does not hold up — the model caught the miscalculated interest totals and the mispriced options and flagged them, finishing about 61% faster. Legal (+14): a commercial lease review, where the win is a refusal — it declined to invent a “standard” market benchmark for renewal terms and notice periods, and flagged what the lease was genuinely missing. Life sciences (+22): writing up a field trial from raw measurement data, getting the sample standard deviations right across both treatment groups where Sonnet 5 missed nearly all of them, about 41% faster. Public sector (+11): a report on a math intervention program, pulling the right student progress-monitoring figures out of the underlying data, about 71% faster. He closes with availability and no date: customers will be able to build agents with Sonnet 5.5 in the Box AI Studio “shortly”. What the numbers cannot tell you: this is Box’s own eval, on Box’s own product surface, published by Box’s CEO.

一条推文，也是今天唯一带对口评测和明确基线的一条。Box 用自己的 “complex work eval” 在 Box Agent 上对 Sonnet 5.5 做了早期访问测试，对比对象是 Sonnet 5。总榜是在最难的那批测试上 +4 分，完成一份交付物的速度约为 2.4 倍，token 少 12%。分行业：金融服务 +18 个百分点，法律 +7，生命科学 +8，公共部门 +7。他随后列了四个单项，幅度都比总榜大得多。金融服务（+48 分）：对一笔交易工具收购做尽职调查，交易手册里的算术对不上，模型抓出了算错的利息合计和定价错位的期权并做了标记，完成速度快约 61%。法律（+14）：一份商业租赁审查，赢面在于它拒绝作答 — 它没有编造一个续租条款与通知期的 “市场标准”，而是指出这份租约真正缺的是什么。生命科学（+22）：用原始测量数据写现场试验报告，两个处理组的样本标准差都算对了，而 Sonnet 5 几乎全错，速度快约 41%。公共部门（+11）：一份数学干预项目的报告，从底层数据里取对了学生进步监测的数值，速度快约 71%。结尾是供给时间但没有日期：客户 “不久” 就能在 Box AI Studio 里用 Sonnet 5.5 搭 agent。这些数字说明不了的是：这是 Box 自己的评测、跑在 Box 自己的产品面上、由 Box 的 CEO 发布。

By axis: eight builders are judging capability, two (Sottiaux on pricing, Rauch on migration and agent access) are reporting adoption, two are DevDay-only, and six have no AI content that can be evaluated.

The Sonnet 5.5 claims are not interchangeable, and the difference is the baseline. Aaron Levie’s figures come from Box’s own eval, run with the Box Agent, against Sonnet 5 — the only numbers today with both a stated comparator and a stated test set. Everything below that is weaker in a specific way. Cat Wu names the comparator (Sonnet 5) but not how “~30% more tasks done” was measured, and her task-level figures (24 seconds, 6K tokens) come from a single run. Boris Cherny’s “30% faster and 30% less usage” does not say whether it describes the model or the one bug-fixing run he is describing. Alex Albert offers two comparators and no numbers. Dan Shipper links a personal benchmark and quotes none of it. Read Levie first and the rest as corroboration rather than replication: four of the five work at Anthropic, and the fifth is evaluating on his own product surface.

One gap inside Levie’s own post is worth carrying forward. The aggregate is +4 points on Box’s hardest tests; the four task-level wins he lists are +48, +22, +14 and +11. Both sets come from the same source, but only the aggregate carries the “hardest tests” qualifier, and the task-level figures are the ones he selected. Separately, one of the four is a win for not producing an answer: in the commercial lease review the model declined to invent a “standard” market benchmark and flagged what the document was actually missing. That is the only item in today’s feed where the improvement consists of a refusal.

Whether mid-tier models still have a place got two answers today, and both are in the same post. Dan Shipper says Sonnet 5.5 is faster and cheaper than Opus 5.5 and is now Kieran Klaassen’s preferred model for quick iterative coding and design work, and that it beats Astra at revision tasks while Astra stays his favourite for writing. In the next breath he reports that kplikethebird and hammer_mt feel they no longer have room in their stack for mid-tier models at all. Neither side gives a number. This digest is not going to pick one.

What the window does contain is the eve-of signals, none of which carry content: Sam Altman’s “We have found a new thing”, Shipper’s “by far the most launches @OpenAI has ever had”, Peter Yang’s first DevDay, Steinberger’s “See ya there!”.

The one new number on the subscription line since 09-06 is Sottiaux’s, and it is about pricing rather than resets. Pro $200 reopens to new subscribers while the usage calculation changes — by his own arithmetic, half the dollar in API spend against the old plan — with a commitment not to bring the 5h limit back. Without it this stays a change in how the plan is calculated, not a measured cut or a measured increase.

核心结论

feed 里又有一次发布了：18 位 builder、34 条推文，其中约三分之二落在两件事之一上。Anthropic 发了 Sonnet 5.5，八位 builder 在 09-28T18:06Z 到 09-29T05:44Z 之间谈它。OpenAI 的 DevDay 在太平洋时间的第二天，五条帖子在 09-28T19:58Z 到 09-29T06:41Z 之间指向它。两件事相隔不到 12 小时，feed 里没有人对两者做直接比较。按轴分：八位在判断能力，两位在讲采用（Sottiaux 讲计价、Rauch 讲迁移与 agent 可及性），两位只有 DevDay，六位没有可评估的 AI 内容。

关于 Sonnet 5.5 的这些说法不能互换，差别在基线。Aaron Levie 的数字来自 Box 自己的评测、跑在 Box Agent 上、对比 Sonnet 5 — 是今天唯一同时写明对比对象和测试集的数字。其余每一条都在某个具体地方更弱。Cat Wu 写明了对比对象（Sonnet 5），但没说 “任务量多约 30%” 怎么测的，她的单项数字（24 秒、6K token）来自单次运行。Boris Cherny 的 “快 30%、用量少 30%” 没说明指的是模型还是他演示的那一次修 bug。Alex Albert 给了两个对比对象但没有数字。Dan Shipper 附了个人 benchmark 却一个数字都没引用。先读 Levie，其余当成佐证而不是复现：五个人里四个在 Anthropic，第五个是在自己的产品面上做评测。

Levie 那条内部有一处落差值得记下来。总榜是 Box 最难那批测试上 +4 分；他列的四个单项是 +48、+22、+14、+11。两组数字同源，但只有总榜带 “最难测试” 这个限定，而单项是他挑出来的。另外，四项里有一项赢在 “不输出答案”：商业租赁审查中，模型拒绝编造一个 “市场标准” 基准，转而指出文件真正缺的是什么。这是今天 feed 里唯一一处 “改进表现为拒绝” 的条目。

中档模型还有没有位置，今天得到了两个答案，而且都在同一条推文里。Dan Shipper 说 Sonnet 5.5 比 Opus 5.5 更快更便宜，已经是 Kieran Klaassen 做快速迭代编码和设计的首选，修改类任务上胜过 Astra，但写作上 Astra 仍是他的最爱。紧接着他又说，kplikethebird 和 hammer_mt 都觉得自己的技术栈里已经完全没有中档模型的位置。两边都没有给数字。这份 digest 不打算替读者选一个。

关于从 09-25 起追踪的那个承诺 — Sottiaux 的 “等不及下周二 DevDay” — 先做窗口核对。本期 feed 快照止于 2026-09-29T06:45Z，也就是太平洋时间 09-28 深夜；DevDay 在太平洋时间 09-29，落在窗口之外。所以这个承诺是仍开着，不是失约，这也是连续第二期给出这个判定。窗口内确实有的是前夜信号，但都不带内容：Sam Altman 的 “我们找到了一个新东西”、Shipper 的 “OpenAI 有史以来发布最多的一次”、Peter Yang 的第一次 DevDay、Steinberger 的 “See ya there!”。

订阅线上自 09-06 以来第一个新数字来自 Sottiaux，但它讲的是计价而不是 resets。Pro $200 重新向新订阅者开放，同时改用量计算 — 按他自己的算式为旧计划一半的 API 花费 — 并承诺不把 5 小时限制加回来。他把原因归结为模型效率以 API 降价的形式传导，举了本周 GPT-6 Sol 与 GPT-6 Luna 降至原价 50%。他没给的是那个能定性的数字：同样 200 美元前后各能买到多少工作量。没有这个数字，它就只是一个计算口径的变化，既不是被测量的削减，也不是被测量的提升。
