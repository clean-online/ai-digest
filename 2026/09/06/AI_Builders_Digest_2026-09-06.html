Claude 现在在桌面端 Claude Cowork 里内置了一个浏览器。当任务需要用到某个网站时，侧边栏会打开一个浏览器，由 Claude 导航页面、阅读、点击、输入——于是你可以把任务里"上网"的那部分交出去，自己留在原地。具体场景：填表、从仪表盘拉数字、或是走通一个没有现成连接器的门户。

No Priors

Redefining Chip Architecture with Arm CEO Rene Haas

The Takeaway Arm CEO Rene Haas argues the CPU is "the heart of everything" and that AI's biggest near-term leverage in chips is verification — turn it off and chip design reverts to 1990s-era productivity. His broader bet: AI as a utility that levels the playing field for every industry, and data centers as job engines, not job killers.
核心结论 Arm CEO Rene Haas 认为 CPU 是"一切的核心"，而 AI 在芯片领域最近期的杠杆是验证环节——把它关掉，芯片设计就会退回 1990 年代的生产力水平。他更大的判断是：AI 是一项"公用事业"，会拉平各行业的起跑线；而数据中心是就业引擎，而非就业杀手。

Rene Haas, CEO of Arm and SoftBank Group International, runs one of the two positions every computing workload ultimately flows through: the CPU. "There's no computing problem that's ever been invented that doesn't utilize and can't utilize the microprocessor. It is the heart of everything. All roads lead through it." His counterintuitive claim is about where AI helps chips most — not design, but the verification, validation, and debug that eat the bulk of a 24-to-36-month cycle. "AI is really good at that. And if we were to shut it off, it's like being in the 1990s" with internet only between 2 and 4pm. "The genie's out of the bottle and there's no stopping that."

On Arm's own move from licensing IP into physical chips, Haas traces it from selling CPU/GPU/system IP to "compute subsystems" (the Lego-blueprint analogy) and finally a physical product — kicked off by Meta wanting a "general purpose agentic CPU" no one else could supply. He's careful to keep the ecosystem on board; Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, and Google all build Arm-based server chips and backed the move.

The strand most relevant to builders: Haas is "a huge believer in AI as a utility that's going to help productivity for every single industry. It is gonna be the great leveler" — healthcare, infrastructure, robotics. Arm uses it heavily on the engineering side for verification, though RTL generation remains immature because models train on public data while chip knowledge is proprietary; they're fine-tuning with model makers to close the gap. On the jobs debate, he's blunt: the fear of AI-driven job loss "is not well grounded at all" — the Electricians Labor Union recently asked not to ban data centers because they need those jobs.

Rene Haas（Arm 及软银集团国际 CEO）掌管着所有计算负载最终都要流经的两个环节之一：CPU。"没有哪个被发明出来的计算问题，是不用、也不能用微处理器的。它是万物之心，条条大路都从它身上穿过。"他反直觉的论点是：AI 对芯片最大的帮助不在设计，而在吞噬了 24–36 个月周期里大部分时间的那个环节——验证、确认与调试。"AI 非常擅长这个。要是把它关掉，就像回到 1990 年代"，只有下午两到四点才有互联网。"精灵已经出了瓶子，挡不住了。"

关于 Arm 从授权 IP 走向自造实体芯片，Haas 把脉络讲得很清楚：从卖 CPU/GPU/系统 IP，到"计算子系统"（乐高蓝图的比喻），再到实体产品——起点是 Meta 想要一颗"通用 agentic CPU"，市面上没人供得了。他格外小心地维系生态：英伟达、亚马逊、微软、谷歌都基于 Arm 造服务器芯片，也都支持这一步。

与 builder 最相关的一条线：Haas"极度相信 AI 会作为一种公用事业，提升每一个行业的生产力。它将成为伟大的均衡器"——医疗、基建、机器人皆然。Arm 在工程侧（尤其是验证）重度使用 AI，不过 RTL 生成仍不成熟，因为模型只学过公开数据，而芯片知识多属专有；他们正与模型方微调以补缺口。谈到就业之争，他直言对 AI 致失业的恐惧"根本站不住脚"——电气工会最近还请求不要禁数据中心，因为他们正需要这些岗位。
