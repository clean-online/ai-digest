Peter Yang Practical AI Tutorials

Peter Yang writes the most structured item in today's feed: a numbered read on the personal agent race, which he labels "armchair." The ranking separates distribution from product. On Meta's Muse: "The app is intuitive, and Meta is aggressively promoting it across all its properties. Once you use the Muse app, you realize that it makes little sense for an agent to live in an existing messaging app like iMessage" — and he expects it to become "the company's most successful homegrown app after Facebook. It will be much bigger than Threads." On ChatGPT: still the leader "because it already has 1B+ users," with models, computer use and voice "arguably better than Muse feature by feature," but "it's hard to build one product for both work and personal use"; he says he started using Grok Bot for work because "the Work/Codex split remains too confusing." He does not read Grok Bot as a Muse competitor at all, since it is work-focused: "My bull case for Grok Bot is that it's a few features away from becoming multiplayer agentic Slack for both bots and humans." Google he calls "obviously the dark horse candidate" — all these agents "live off Gmail, Google Calendar, and your Google app's data," while Google's own Spark is "a secondary tab in Gemini." Then the column nobody has filled: "Speaking of multiplayer, nobody has figured this out yet. I can't easily add my spouse to a Muse chat to plan a vacation." His own stack is Muse for personal, Grok Bot for cloud tasks, Claude for specific use cases and ChatGPT for everything else, and his closing advice is about portability: "Everyone should design their personal skills and files so they're easy to port between harnesses and agents."

Peter Yang 写出了今天 feed 里最有结构的一条：一份编号式的个人 agent 竞赛解读，他自己标注为"armchair"。这份排名把分发和产品分开看。关于 Meta 的 Muse："这个 app 很直观，而且 Meta 正在它所有产品里大力推它。你一旦用过 Muse app，就会意识到让一个 agent 住在 iMessage 这种现有消息应用里，其实说不通"——他预计它会成为"这家公司在 Facebook 之后最成功的自研 app，会比 Threads 大得多"。关于 ChatGPT：它仍是领先者"因为它已经有 10 亿以上用户"，模型、computer use 和语音"逐项对比大概都优于 Muse"，但"很难用一个产品同时覆盖工作和个人用途"；他说自己开始用 Grok Bot 处理工作，是因为"Work/Codex 的拆分还是太让人困惑"。他完全不把 Grok Bot 当成 Muse 的竞品，因为它聚焦工作："我对 Grok Bot 的看多理由是，它再差几个功能，就会变成同时给 bot 和人用的多人协作版 agentic Slack。"Google 他称为" obviously 的黑马候选"——这些个人 agent 都"靠 Gmail、Google Calendar 和你 Google app 的数据活着"，而 Google 自己的 Spark 目前"只是 Gemini 里的一个次级 tab"。然后是还没有人填上那一栏："说到多人协作，目前没人解决。我没法轻松把我的配偶加进一个 Muse 对话里去规划度假。"他自己的组合是 Muse 管个人、Grok Bot 跑云端任务、Claude 处理特定用例、ChatGPT 兜底其他；结尾的建议关于可移植性："每个人都应该把自己的 personal skills 和文件设计成便于在 harness 与 agent 之间迁移，否则你会有一半时间花在从一个工具搬到另一个工具上。"

The Takeaway

The personal agent race got its first real scorecard today. Peter Yang's numbered read is the first attempt in this feed to rank the field rather than champion one product, and it separates distribution from product quality: ChatGPT leads on users ("1B+"), Muse leads on product intuition plus Meta's promotion machine, Grok Bot is quietly aiming at work rather than at personal use, and Google is the dark horse that owns the data these agents run on while leaving its own Spark as "a secondary tab in Gemini." The one column nobody has filled is multiplayer — "I can't easily add my spouse to a Muse chat to plan a vacation." His parting advice, that personal skills and files should be portable across harnesses, is the practical version of the same observation: the field is not settled enough to bet your setup on one winner.

The capability side moved in the same direction. Rauch's post is a small story with a large claim — the agent did not propose a fix, it reproduced the bug, simulated it, stood up an ephemeral deployment, and verified against an iPhone simulator. What he says humans cannot match is not cleverness but grind: "We'd run of energy, we'd extrapolate, we'd hope it'd work out." That is the verifier argument he made on 09-16, this time with an instance attached to it.

Two smaller signals bracket the day. Jev keeps compounding: a Vercel release on 09-18, usage numbers on 09-20, and now a dedicated Latent Space episode — which Matt Turck immediately deflates by pointing out that Instinct had its week only days earlier. And Steinberger's "your claw can now FaceTime you" closes, from a different product, the question Nan Yu left open on 09-21 about whether agents can place calls. Yesterday it was a capability being asked about; today it is one being shipped.

核心结论

个人 agent 竞赛今天拿到了第一份像样的评分表。Peter Yang 那份编号解读，是这份 feed 里第一次试图给整个赛道排序，而不是替某一个产品站台；它把分发和产品分开看：ChatGPT 赢在用户（"1B+"），Muse 赢在产品直觉加上 Meta 的推广机器，Grok Bot 悄悄瞄的是工作场景而非个人场景，Google 是握着这些 agent 所依赖数据、却把自己的 Spark 只放在"Gemini 里一个次级 tab"的黑马。唯一还没人填上的是多人协作那一栏——"我没法轻松把我的配偶加进一个 Muse 对话里去规划度假"。他结尾那句"personal skills 和文件要便于跨 harness 迁移"是同一个观察的实用版：这个赛道远没到可以把自己的配置押在某一个赢家身上的程度。

能力侧朝着同一个方向推进。Rauch 那条是个小题材、大主张——agent 没有只是提出一个修复方案，而是复现了 bug、做了模拟、搭了一个临时部署、再对着 iPhone 模拟器验证。他说人类匹配不了的并不是聪明，而是死磕："我们会没电，我们会靠推断，我们会指望它刚好能work。"这正是他在 09-16 提出的 verifier 论点，只不过这次附上了一个实例。

当天两端还有两个更小的信号。Jev 仍在叠加：09-18 是 Vercel 的发布，09-20 是使用量，现在是一期专门的 Latent Space 节目——Matt Turck 立刻给它降温，指出 Instinct 的热度也就在几天之前。而 Steinberger 那句"你的 claw 现在可以 FaceTime 你"，从另一个产品那边回应了 Nan Yu 在 09-21 留下的、关于 agent 能不能打电话的问题：昨天是一个被追问的能力，今天是一个已经上线的能力。
