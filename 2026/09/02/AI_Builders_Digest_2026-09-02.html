Training Data

Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed: Why AI Models Stop Learning, and How to Start It Again

The Takeaway Rich Sutton (father of reinforcement learning, author of “The Bitter Lesson,” cofounder of Oak Lab with former student Khurram Javed) argues the field has it backwards: “I'm not weird, you are.” The core claim — all learning is continual, but today's LLMs freeze their weights at launch and never learn again. The path forward is continual deep learning: letting agents learn from their own experience in the infinitely complex “big world,” instead of being bottlenecked by human-curated synthetic data. Oak Lab's ambition is a self-consistent mind that learns across the full spectrum, tiny to big.

The Bitter Lesson, refined.. Sutton rewrites it in 26 words: don't be distracted by human knowledge; focus on learning methods that scale with computation — like search and learning. LLMs are both a positive and a negative example: they proved massive compute-scaling works, then hit a wall because the internet (finite human data) eventually runs out.

The Big World Hypothesis.. Synthetic data is, in Sutton's words, “a big mistake” — it's bottlenecked by humans who must decide what good data even is (his echolocation-drone example: no domain expert, no data). The world is infinitely more complex than any simulation; the only scalable source of learning is the agent's own experience.

Why LLMs don't really learn.. Their weights never change after launch. In-context learning and memory are state, not learning. Cursor's tab/autocomplete is a rare true example of continual learning (weights update from use) — but done inefficiently via huge shared batches that blend everyone's lessons.

The algorithmic gap: catastrophic forgetting.. Naive weight updates destroy prior knowledge. The cure: per-weight step-size optimization (meta-learned, so most weights barely move) plus generate-and-test in feature space — “continual backprop” (published in Nature), which keeps injecting random seed units so the network can keep growing.

Oak Lab's ambition.. The Alberta Plan's 12 steps; step 2 (continual deep learning) unlocks the rest, then getting abstractions right. The endgame: a self-consistent, self-training mind spanning sensorimotor to high-level abstraction, treated uniformly — Sutton's “within reach” vision of a trillion-parameter model at 20 watts in 5–10 years.

核心结论 Rich Sutton（强化学习之父、《苦涩的教训》作者，与前学生 Khurram Javed 共同创立 Oak Lab）认为整个领域本末倒置了：“不是我怪，是你们怪。”核心论点——一切学习都是持续的，但今天的 LLM 在发布时冻结权重、从此不再学习。出路是持续深度学习：让 agent 从自身经验、从无限复杂的“大世界”中学习，而非被人类策展的合成数据所瓶颈。Oak Lab 的野心，是一颗能贯穿“从微小到宏大”全谱、自我维持一致性的心智。

《苦涩的教训》再提炼。. Sutton 把它重写成了 26 个词：别被人类知识分心；专注那些能随算力扩展的学习方法——比如搜索与学习。LLM 既是一个正面、也是一个反面例子：它们证明了大规模算力扩展确实有效，但当互联网（有限的人类数据）终究耗尽时，便撞上了墙。

大世界假说。. 用 Sutton 的话说，合成数据是“一个大错误”——它被人所瓶颈，必须有人判定什么才算好数据（他举的回声定位无人机例子：没有领域专家，就没有数据）。世界比任何模拟都无限复杂；唯一可扩展的学习来源，是 agent 自身的经验。

为什么 LLM 并没有真正学习。. 它们的权重在发布后永不再变。上下文学习与记忆是“状态”，不是学习。Cursor 的 tab / 自动补全是少见的真正持续学习例子（权重随使用更新）——但做法低效，靠把所有人的经验混进巨型共享批次来更新。

算法鸿沟：灾难性遗忘。. 朴素的权重更新会摧毁已有知识。解药是：逐权重的步长优化（通过元学习，使多数权重几乎不动）加上特征空间里的“生成—测试”——“持续反向传播（continual backprop）”（发表于 Nature），持续注入随机种子单元，让网络能不断生长。

Oak Lab 的野心。. Alberta 计划的 12 个步骤；第 2 步（持续深度学习）解锁其余，然后是“把抽象搞对”。终局：一颗自我一致、自我训练的心智，从感官运动到高层抽象一以贯之——这是 Sutton 眼中“触手可及”的愿景：5–10 年内，用 20 瓦跑一万亿参数模型。
