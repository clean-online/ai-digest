Guillermo Rauch Vercel CEO

“Reject non-understanding.” His argument: slop grenades are not only code and pull requests, and the real risk is that reading gets entirely discounted, because of the exhaustion of low-quality unverified AI prose being thrown at you all the time. The example he cites is a thread going viral about a performance improvement attributed to a compiler change, where the pull request description itself has the AI saying the gain is not from the compiler change but from how the algorithms and data structures changed — with the implication that a similar win could have been attained otherwise. He closes on what he wants instead: AI in the service of understanding the universe and enhancing human cognition and creativity.

The Takeaway

The longest thread this feed has been carrying closes today, and it closes with a sentence rather than a figure. Resets moved four times: 09-11, not applying in ChatGPT Work and Codex, with a make-good and an apology; 09-24, a banked reset loaded as a promised change; 09-27, a reset as the remedy for an outage; today, “Resets all propagated.” What was missing each time is still missing now — no account count, no quota, no window. What stays open is the DevDay he dated to “next Tuesday” on 09-25; it is absent from this snapshot.

The second thing is that ceilings moved at two labs within hours, and neither report is measurable. Sottiaux’s is an operator’s completion notice; Peter Yang’s is a user saying Claude limits went from barely usable to basically unlimited, while writing that he does not know what happened. Two anecdotes on the same axis are still two anecdotes. By the rule this digest has applied since 09-25, a report from the person using the product cannot settle a capacity question, and by the axis test added on 09-26, neither of these is a capacity measurement in the first place. What the feed supports is narrower than “capacity grew”: two people’s limits moved, and neither said by how much.

The most argued position of the day points at reading itself. Rauch’s “Reject non-understanding” targets AI prose, and the case he uses is a performance win credited to a compiler when the pull request text says the win came from algorithms and data structures. The same caution lands on the two items above: a completion notice and a limits anecdote both read like measurements, and neither is one. That is the one thing worth carrying out of a feed this thin.

The rest of the feed is thinner than those three and is rendered rather than dropped, so the count matches what the feed carried: a birth announcement, two lines of slogan, a community-organising post, an “AGI” reaction whose object the feed never names, a one-line “go outside”, a one-year video-making marker, and two scenes of a Plato film posted without any evaluation of them. 8 builders, 14 tweets, and one podcast that was already covered on 09-20.

核心结论

本 feed 追踪得最久的那条线今天收口了，而且是用一句话收的，不是用一个数字。Resets 动了四次：09-11 在 ChatGPT Work 与 Codex 里没有生效，附带补偿与致歉；09-24 一次 banked reset 作为承诺过的变更被载入；09-27 reset 成为一次故障的补救；今天「Resets 全部生效了」。每次都缺的东西现在仍然缺 —— 没有账户数、没有额度、没有时间窗。仍然开着的是他 09-25 定在「下周二」的 DevDay；它在这份快照里缺席。

第二件事是两家实验室的上限在几小时内都被抬高了，而两份报告都不可度量。Sottiaux 那条是运营方的完成通知；Peter Yang 那条是一个用户说 Claude 的限额从勉强能用变成基本无限，同时写着他不知道发生了什么。同一条轴上的两条轶事仍然是两条轶事。按本日报自 09-25 起沿用的规则，来自产品使用方的一份报告了结不了一个产能问题；再按 09-26 加上的那条「轴」的检验，这两条从一开始就不是产能测量。feed 能支撑的说法比「产能增长了」更窄：两个人的限额动了，而且谁都没说动了多少。

今天论证最用力的一条指向的是「阅读」本身。Rauch 的「拒绝不理解」针对的是 AI 生成的文字，而他用的案例是一项性能提升被记在编译器账上，可 PR 文本自己说增益来自算法和数据结构。同样的谨慎落在上面那两条上：一条完成通知和一条限额轶事读起来都像测量，而两者都不是。这是一份这么薄的 feed 里唯一值得带走的东西。

feed 的其余部分比这三条更薄，照常渲染而不是删掉，这样数字与 feed 承载的内容一致：一条新生儿公告、两行口号、一条社区动员帖、一条 feed 从未点明对象的「AGI」反应、一行「今天出门了」、一个一年的做视频标记，以及一部柏拉图短剧的两个场景（发了但没有任何评价）。8 位 builder、14 条推文，以及一期已于 09-20 收录过的播客。
