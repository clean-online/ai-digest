Aaron Levie Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie writes the day's only complete argument, and it is about price. "The frontier models just became substantially cheaper, with the Opus 5.5 price cuts, and now with GPT-6 Sol and Luna dropping token prices by 50%. The rate at which the cost per task (on a like-for-like basis) drops in AI is unlike any other type of technology in history." The mechanism he names is Jevons paradox: every time the cost drops, "the use-cases you can deploy agents against dramatically increase" — processing all of your data, scanning code for security issues, reading through log data to make decisions, agent swarms in workflows. One thing to keep separate: this is not the model named Jev that Vercel shipped last week and that ran through the 09-18 and 09-20 issues. Similar sound, unrelated thing.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 写了今天唯一一段完整的论证，而它讲的是价格。"前沿模型刚刚变得大幅便宜——Opus 5.5 降价，现在 GPT-6 Sol 和 Luna 又把 token 价格砍掉 50%。AI 里'单位任务的成本'（在同等可比基础上）下降的速度，历史上没有任何一类技术是这样的。"他点出的机制是 Jevons paradox：成本每降一次，"你值得把 agent 投进去的用例就大幅增加"——处理全部数据、扫代码找安全问题、把日志读完来做决策、工作流里跑 agent 集群。有一点要分开：这不是 Vercel 上周发布的那个叫 Jev 的模型，那个名字出现在 09-18 和 09-20 两期里。读音相近，两回事。

His second post is where the claim gets tested, and it is the only place in today's feed with task-level numbers. Box ran Opus 5.5 against Opus 5 on complex enterprise knowledge work over unstructured data: 63% fewer tokens used, 42% less verbosity, 30% faster. Then four industry cases. Financial services due diligence, +39% task accuracy on 82% fewer tokens — a perfect result on every attempt, in half the words. Technology cloud cost analysis, +65% on 70% fewer tokens, and he says why it holds: the model "kept the source data's unit conventions straight all the way through", so the number at the end stands up. Consumer products client account analysis, +17% on 78% fewer, where the contract never states a senior/junior split and the model derived it from an 18-person roster and showed the rule it used. Clinical diagnostics, +15% on 78% fewer, where it caught that the two groups' standard deviations differed more than 100-fold, re-ran the test the right way, and found the dry-season difference "didn't hold up after all". Three of the four wins are efficiency. The fourth is the model changing the conclusion.

他的第二条是这个主张被检验的地方，也是今天这份 feed 里唯一有任务级数字的地方。Box 用 Opus 5.5 对 Opus 5，在非结构化数据上的复杂企业知识工作里跑了一遍：token 用量少 63%、啰嗦程度低 42%、快 30%。然后是四个行业用例。金融服务尽调，任务准确率 +39%，token 少 82%——每一次尝试都是满分结果，字数只用一半。科技行业云成本分析，+65%，token 少 70%，他还解释了为什么这个结果站得住：模型"一路把源数据的单位口径保持住了"，所以最后那个数字是成立的。消费品客户账户分析，+17%，token 少 78%——合同里从没写资深/初级怎么分，模型从一份 18 人名单里推了出来，并且把它用的规则摆了出来。临床诊断数据分析，+15%，token 少 78%——它发现两组标准差相差超过 100 倍，用正确方式重跑了一遍，结论是旱季的差异"最后并没有成立"。四项里三项赢在效率，第四项是模型改变了结论。

Alex Albert Research, Anthropic

Anthropic's Alex Albert builds a world instead of arguing about one: "I've been on a Blender kick with Opus 5.5. The Takeaway

Two frontier releases landed on the same day, and both led with price. Anthropic made Opus 5.5 the default in Claude Code and the Claude app, with Cat Wu setting effort to medium — "comparable to Fable 5.1 on intelligence but faster" — and rate limits going 25% further than Opus 5. OpenAI shipped GPT-6 Sol and Luna with a permanent 50% API price cut. Aaron Levie, who buys from both sides, is the one who says what that means: the cost per task on a like-for-like basis drops "unlike any other type of technology in history", and every drop opens up more use-cases worth pointing agents at. He gives it a name: Jevons paradox applied to agents.

The numbers behind his claim are the most concrete thing in the feed, and they all point one way. Box ran Opus 5.5 against Opus 5 on enterprise knowledge work: 63% fewer tokens, 42% less verbosity, 30% faster. Four industry tests, four gains — due diligence +39% task accuracy on 82% fewer tokens, cloud cost analysis +65% on 70% fewer, client account analysis +17% on 78% fewer, clinical diagnostics +15% on 78% fewer. Three are efficiency wins. The fourth is different in kind: the model noticed two groups' standard deviations differed more than 100-fold, re-ran the test, and the dry-season difference "didn't hold up after all". Cheaper tokens changed an answer, not just a bill.

What the cheaper token gets spent on is where the day splits. Boris Cherny spends it on correctness: a couple of short prompts in Lean produced 16 PRs fixing bugs and race conditions in the Claude Agent SDK, in a language he says he does not know well, and he leaves the question open — "Is formal verification the future of coding (or at least, bug finding)?" Thariq spends it on restraint: "not to ship 10x more features to prod", but on understanding users, running experiments, building prototypes. Same premise about capability getting cheaper, opposite conclusions about what to do with it. Rauch's own evals sit in the middle of that argument: Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol and Fable 5.1 all at 97%, Grok 4.7 at 94%, and his only comment is that Grok is 2x-7x cheaper.

One thread from an earlier issue closed today. On 09-23 this feed carried Sottiaux's tease — "We are almost Tuesday and I promised a reset for Tuesday" — which followed his 09-11 disclosure that resets were not fully applying in ChatGPT Work and Codex, with a make-good and an apology. The reset arrived on the day he named: "We are loading a banked reset into all accounts of our Plus, Pro and Business users." Two things worth keeping straight. The reset and the models are what landed, not the consumer agent Nikunj reported as imminent yesterday — there is no agent announcement in this feed. And the independent benchmark gap has not closed: the only cross-model number today is Vercel running its own Next.js eval.

核心结论

同一天落地了两个前沿发布，而两边都把价格放在最前面。Anthropic 把 Opus 5.5 设为 Claude Code 和 Claude 应用的默认模型，Cat Wu 把 effort 定为 medium——"智能水平和 Fable 5.1 相当，但更快"——速率额度比 Opus 5 多走 25%。OpenAI 发布 GPT-6 Sol 和 Luna，API 价格永久性下调 50%。两边都买的 Aaron Levie 是那个说出这意味着什么的人：在同等可比基础上，单位任务的成本下降速度"历史上没有任何一类技术是这样的"，而每一次下降都会打开更多值得把 agent 投进去的用例。他给了它一个名字：Jevons paradox 用在 agent 上。

支撑他这个主张的数字是这份 feed 里最具体的东西，而且全部指向同一个方向。Box 用 Opus 5.5 对 Opus 5 跑企业知识工作：token 少 63%、啰嗦程度低 42%、快 30%。四个行业测试，四项增益——尽调任务准确率 +39%、token 少 82%；云成本分析 +65%、token 少 70%；客户账户分析 +17%、token 少 78%；临床诊断 +15%、token 少 78%。三项是效率上的赢。第四项性质不同：模型发现两组标准差相差超过 100 倍，重跑了检验，结论是旱季的差异"最后并没有成立"。更便宜的 token 改变的是一个答案，不只是一张账单。

便宜下来的 token 花在什么地方，是今天分歧所在。Boris Cherny 花在正确性上：几个很短的 prompt 配 Lean，产出 16 个 PR 修掉 Claude Agent SDK 里的 bug 和竞态条件，而他说自己并不熟这门语言，最后他把问题留在那里——"形式化验证会是编码的未来吗（或者至少，是找 bug 的未来）？"Thariq 花在克制上："不是往生产环境多推 10 倍功能"，而是去理解用户、做实验、搭原型。前提相同（能力在变便宜），结论相反（该拿它做什么）。Rauch 自己的评测正落在这场争论的中间：Opus 5.5、GPT-6 Sol、Fable 5.1 全是 97%，Grok 4.7 是 94%，而他唯一的评语是 Grok 便宜 2 到 7 倍。

前几期的一条线今天收口了。09-23 那期 feed 里是 Sottiaux 的预告——"我们快到周二了，我答应过周二会有一次 reset"——而这条预告又接在他 09-11 的披露之后：resets 在 ChatGPT Work 与 Codex 中使用时没有完全生效，他给出了补偿和致歉。这次 reset 在他点名的那天到了："我们正在向所有 Plus、Pro 和 Business 用户的账户里补入一次 banked reset。"两件事要分清楚。落地的是这次 reset 和这两个模型，不是 Nikunj 昨天说即将到来的那个消费级 agent——这份 feed 里没有任何 agent 发布的消息。而独立基准测试的缺口也没有补上：今天唯一的跨模型数字，是 Vercel 自己跑的 Next.js eval。
