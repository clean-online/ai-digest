Cat Wu Claude Code & Cowork, Anthropic

Cat Wu of Claude Code and Cowork at Anthropic supplies the reason, and it is about routing: "Many of you shared that you didn't want to choose which Claude product to use for any task. As Claude models have become more capable, we've found that Claude can handle the routing for you. Based on your prompt, Claude will decide whether to give a quick answer or do deeper agentic work, and what kind of output best fits the task." She draws the control boundary explicitly: "You're in control the whole time: you can still stop, redirect, or exert finer control over Claude's effort or approach." Her post is the clearest statement in the feed of the trade being made — the model decides the depth of work, in exchange for not making the user pick a product first.

Anthropic 负责 Claude Code 与 Cowork 的 Cat Wu 给出的是理由，而这个理由关于路由："你们很多人说过，不想为了某个任务去选该用哪一个 Claude 产品。随着 Claude 模型变得更强大，我们发现路由这件事 Claude 可以自己处理。根据你的提示，Claude 会判断该给一个快速回答，还是做更深的 agentic 工作，以及哪种输出形式最适合这个任务。"她也把控制的边界明确画了出来："全程你都掌握着控制权：你仍然可以叫停、转向，或者对 Claude 投入的精力和采取的方式做更细的控制。"放量节奏是"在未来几周内慢慢来，把它做对"。她这条是 feed 里对这笔交易说得最清楚的一条——由模型来决定工作的深度，换来的是用户不必先挑一个产品。

The Takeaway

Today's feed is one event with four accounts posting it: chat and Cowork become one Claude, and Docs, Slides and Design move into the conversation. The mechanism Cat Wu names is the part that matters beyond this launch — routing is now the model's job, so "Claude will decide whether to give a quick answer or do deeper agentic work, and what kind of output best fits the task." Boris Cherny's history of it is the same move described from the other end: first developers stopped writing the code, then knowledge workers stopped assembling the files, and now the product holding both collapses into one surface. On the evidence of the blog, the trigger was user behaviour, not capability — "People used both, and told us the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged."

The second thread is cheap and fast becoming its own category, and it shows up twice with opposite valuations. Rauch's version has a number and an architecture: a safety reviewer "analyzing every command" running on GPT Luna, with Jev "up to 18x faster (p95) and more accurate," heading for Vercel AI Gateway "and likely new default." Shipper's version of the same name is a joke with a red flag on it — "jev is just a JSON classifier." Levie supplies the demand side for whichever reading is right: what you spend that on is "data classification tasks, routing decisions inside of a workflow," and "quick judgment calls about safety or security," which he calls "the gates in a large number of processes."

Underneath both, the plumbing changed. Thariq's three posts say stop making the shell the universal adapter — "give Claude tools shaped in the way you want vs tricking it with a layer of indirection" — while conceding that "sandboxes + bash are still good for work that involves code generation & execution." Garry Tan pushes the same decoupling one layer up: "I can use any harness I want and it's the same personality and full memory of my personal AGI."

Two notes against the grain. Zara Zhang says she is "using Claude less these days because the way it speaks has just become insufferable" — a tone complaint landing on the same day the announcement is about simplicity, which is a reminder that capability and voice are now separate product problems. And Sam Altman says the main launch he was excited about slips "to next week instead," so the cadence comparison for the day is Anthropic collapsing surfaces while OpenAI defers.

核心结论

今天的 feed 是同一件事、四个账号在发：chat 和 Cowork 合成一个 Claude，Docs、Slides 和 Design 走进对话。其中超出本次发布本身、更值得记住的是 Cat Wu 点出的机制——路由现在归模型管，所以"Claude 会判断该给一个快速回答，还是做更深的 agentic 工作，以及哪种输出形式最适合这个任务"。Boris Cherny 讲的那段历史，是同一件事从另一头说的：先是开发者不再自己写代码，然后知识工作者不再自己拼文件，现在承载这两件事的产品收敛成了一个界面。按官方博客给的说法，触发这次改动的是用户行为而不是能力——"两个都有人在用，而他们告诉我们，最烦的是要决定一个任务该属于哪里。"

第二条主线是"又快又便宜"正在成为独立品类，而它今天出现了两次，估值方向相反。Rauch 的版本有数字也有架构：一个"逐条分析命令"的安全审查器跑在 GPT Luna 上，而 Jev"快了 18 倍（p95）而且更准"，将会进入 Vercel AI Gateway"而且很可能成为新的默认"。Shipper 对同一个名字的版本是个插了红旗的玩笑——"jev 不过是个 JSON 分类器"。无论哪个读法对，Levie 提供了需求侧的答案：这些算力该花在"数据分类任务、工作流内部的路由决策"以及"关于安全或安保的快速判断"上，而他把这些称为"大量流程里的关卡"。

在这两条之下，管道层也变了。Thariq 那三条的意思是别再让 shell 当万能适配器——"直接把工具做成你想要的样子交给 Claude，而不是用一层间接去骗它"——同时也承认"对于涉及代码生成与执行的工作，沙箱 + bash 依然很好用"。Garry Tan 把同样的解耦又往上推了一层："我可以用任何我想要的 harness，而它还是同一个性格，带着我那个个人 AGI 的完整记忆。"

还有两条反向的注脚。Zara Zhang 说自己"这些天用 Claude 变少了，因为它说话的方式已经让人难以忍受"——一条关于语气的抱怨，恰好落在宣布"简化"的同一天，提醒了一件事：能力和说话方式现在是两个独立的产品问题。而 Sam Altman 说他最期待的发布"改到下周了"，所以这一天的节奏对照是：Anthropic 在收敛界面，OpenAI 在推迟发布。

Anthropic's post states the reason for the merge in one sentence, and it is about the user's decision cost rather than the model: "We built Cowork as a separate place for bigger work, and Design for visual work. People used both, and told us the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged. What they'd started in one also didn't carry into the other. So we stopped making you choose." The scope sentence is the operational one — "It's rolling out on Pro and Max plans over the next few weeks, with more plans to follow" — with "Team and Free plans will follow soon," and Enterprise admins getting "at least 30 days" notice. The three new surfaces are "Claude Docs and Claude Slides are new today, and Claude Design now works inside your conversations too," all "in beta on paid plans," and "Enterprise admins choose when to turn them on."

Anthropic 这篇用一句话讲清了合并的理由，而这个理由关乎用户的决策成本，不是模型："我们当初把 Cowork 做成一个做大事的地方，把 Design 做成一个做视觉的地方。两个都有人在用，而他们告诉我们，最烦的是要决定一个任务该属于哪里。在其中一个里开了头，也带不进另一个。所以我们不再让你选。"真正有运营含义的是范围那句——"未来几周内在 Pro 和 Max 套餐上开放，更多套餐随后跟进"——"Team 和 Free 套餐很快跟进"，而企业管理员会提前至少 30 天收到通知。三个新界面是"Claude Docs 和 Claude Slides 今天上线，Claude Design 现在也能在你的对话里工作"，全部"在付费套餐上处于 beta"，并且"由企业管理员决定何时开启"。

Two details are worth pulling out. First, the autonomy default: "By default, Claude asks before taking an action.
