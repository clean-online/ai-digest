The Takeaway

The promise tracked since 09-25 closes today. Sottiaux's “Can't wait for DevDay next Tuesday” pointed at 09-29 US Pacific time, and this feed's snapshot ends at 2026-09-30T06:45Z, which is 23:45 Pacific on 09-29, so the whole of DevDay is inside the window for the first time. It happened: Sottiaux writes of connecting with people in person at DevDay at 01:09 UTC on 09-30, and Peter Yang says he had a great time there while attending no session other than the keynotes. Two independent first-hand confirmations, from people standing on opposite sides of the building. Verdict: delivered, after two issues of still open.

What came out of it, from the feed only. Dots, announced by Altman as a new way to use AI that works 24/7 for you; 6.1 Sol, priced at one fifth of Astra with a 95% cache read discount; something called Ultrafast, described as fast and nothing else; and from Swyx standing at the venue, CUA and the Decisions API as two more topics of the day. That is four or five items depending on whether Ultrafast counts, which lines up with Shipper's eve-of-DevDay line that this would be by far the most launches OpenAI has ever had. That claim now has a launch list behind it instead of nothing.

Two numbers in the entire feed, and both are prices. Altman's one fifth and 95% have a named comparison object in Astra and a defined unit, which puts them above everything else here, but they are the vendor's own figures, they answer what it costs rather than how good it is, and the price of Astra itself is never stated, so the ratio cannot be checked from the feed. Everything else that sounds like a measurement is not one: Sottiaux's few million dots is a projection, his felt a jump after two or three days is n=1 and self reported, Rauch's 30M weekly downloads counts downloads rather than quality, and Altman's Ultrafast is so fast names a product and measures nothing. Ranked by which question each one answers, Rauch's download count and Altman's price pair are the only two that could in principle be verified; the rest are a demo, an opinion, or a forecast.

Dots is the one launch with a stated hold on it. Sottiaux says the ability to create an entire team of dots is not out yet and gives the reason, that they are learning from how people use their primary dot first. His account of when it got better is worth separating from the launch itself, because the improvement he reports came after two to three days of teaching it his preferences, not on arrival. Anyone reading a few million dots online within days as an adoption figure should note it is a forecast from the person shipping it.

Astra is now the reference point from both sides, three days apart. On 09-30 Shipper rated Sonnet 5.5 above Astra for revision tasks while keeping Astra as his favourite for writing; today Altman prices 6.1 Sol against Astra at one fifth. Two vendors, one rival used as the yardstick, and no benchmark in either case. The comparison is being made in price and in taste rather than in measured output, which is worth holding on to when either number gets repeated.

核心结论

从 09-25 开始追踪的那个承诺今天收口。Sottiaux 的 “等不及下周二 DevDay” 指的是太平洋时间 09-29，而本期 feed 快照止于 2026-09-30T06:45Z，即太平洋时间 09-29 的 23:45，整个 DevDay 第一次完整落在窗口内。它确实发生了：Sottiaux 在 09-30 01:09 UTC 写到在 DevDay 和很多人当面见了；Peter Yang 说他在那里玩得很开心，除了 keynote 一场分会都没参加。两个相互独立的现场确认，来自立场不同的两个人。判定：兑现，此前连续两期是仍开着。

从 feed 里能看到发了什么。Dots，Altman 称其为一种 24/7 为你工作的新用法；6.1 Sol，定价为 Astra 的五分之一，另有 95% cache read 折扣；一个叫 Ultrafast 的东西，除了快没有别的描述；还有 Swyx 在现场点出的 CUA 和 Decisions API 两个当天主题。算不算 Ultrafast 决定是四项还是五项，这也对上了 Shipper 在 DevDay 前夜那句 “OpenAI 有史以来发布最多的一次”。那句话现在终于有了发布清单作支撑，而不是空口。

整个 feed 只有两个数字，而且都是价格。Altman 的五分之一和 95% 有点名的比较对象 Astra 和明确单位，因此排在这里所有内容之前，但它们是厂商自己的数字，回答的是多少钱而不是多好，而且 Astra 本身的价格从未给出，所以这个比值在 feed 里无法核对。其他听起来像测量的都不是：Sottiaux 的几百万个 dot 是预期；他两三天后有跃升是 n=1 的自述；Rauch 的每周 3,000 万下载是数下载次数而不是质量；Altman 的 Ultrafast 太快了点了个产品名、什么也没测。按各自回答的问题排序，Rauch 的下载数和 Altman 的那一对价格是仅有的两个原则上可核实的；其余是一个 demo、一段观点或一个预期。
