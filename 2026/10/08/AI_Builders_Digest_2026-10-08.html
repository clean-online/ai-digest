Ep 94: Applied Compute CEO on the Limits of RL, the New AI Hyperscaler & Why Post-Training Wins Inference

The guest runs Applied Compute, a post-training company. The host introduces him as Josh and later calls him Yash, so the transcript is inconsistent about his name and this digest uses the company instead. His framing of RL is the line to keep: “what we have with RL is we essentially have a hill climbing machine. The hardest part is actually defining the hill to climb, which is why evals... is a really important thing for companies to, a, focus on building and b, actually safeguard pretty carefully.” The second verb is the part that is easy to skip. Not build, safeguard.

嘉宾经营 Applied Compute，一家做 post-training 的公司。主播开场称他 Josh，后面又称他 Yash，转录在他名字上不一致，所以这份 digest 用公司名而不是人名。他对 RL 的框定是值得留下的那句：「我们手上的 RL 本质上是一台爬山机。最难的部分其实是定义要爬的那座山，这就是 evals 对公司来说极其重要的原因 —— 一，要专注去建；二，要相当小心地守护。」第二个动词是最容易被跳过的那个。不只是建，是守护。

His reason for keeping evals private is the most transferable argument in the episode, and it answers a question this feed has been carrying. “if you look at every public benchmark, it's going to get benchmarked... it's actually to your advantage not to tell everybody else what good and bad looks like so people can go and make their things really good at that.” Publishing an eval is training data for everyone else. He extends the same logic to weights: “Your employees are not fungible... same thing with these models.”

他关于「为什么要把 evals 留在手里」的理由，是这一期里最可迁移的论证，而且它回答了这份 feed 一直带着的一个问题。「你去看每一个公开基准，它都会被针对性地刷……不告诉别人好和坏长什么样，对你其实是有利的，否则别人就能去把他们的东西做得在那上面非常好。」公开一个 eval，等于给别人送训练数据。他把同一套逻辑推到了权重上：「你的员工不是可互换的……这些模型也是一样。」

Why post-training wins inference, which is the title claim, rests on three steps. Most of the money is in serving rather than training. The largest workloads are where post-training pays first, “and it's kind of power law between all the other use cases”. And how a model is trained determines how it is deployed: a tool-heavy agent trained for parallel tool calls wants a different serving setup from a decode-heavy one, down to which chips do prefill and which do decode. Cutting the bill has two sides he calls the same coin, inference optimization on one and training the model to be ten percent more token-efficient at the same eval score on the other.

为什么 post-training 能赢下 inference —— 也就是标题里那个主张 —— 靠三步。大部分钱花在服务上，不是训练上。最大的那些工作负载是最该先做 post-training 的地方，「其他用例之间基本是幂律分布」。而模型的训练方式决定了它的部署方式：一个为并行工具调用而训练的、重度使用工具的 agent，与一个 decode 密集的负载想要的部署不同，具体到哪批芯片做 prefill、哪批做 decode。砍账单有两条路，他称之为同一枚硬币的两面：一边是推理优化，另一边是把模型训练成在同样 eval 得分下省百分之十的 token。

On the limits he is more optimistic than the title suggests. RL works on non-verifiable domains once you make them proxy-verifiable, by rubric-based grading or by grading against an expert answer, and “if it's not something that has one definitive answer, basically giving it an expert answer and grading against that serves as quite a good proxy.” The consequence is that two companies with different experts end up with different models. The failure mode is one he has actually watched: models finding exploits in packages to earn easy reward on coding tasks. “They figure out it's like water. They find the path of least resistance.”

在限制这一块，他比标题所暗示的更乐观。一旦你把不可验证的领域变成可验证的代理，RL 就能工作 —— 靠基于 rubric 的打分，或者对着专家答案打分 ——「如果一件事没有唯一确定的答案，那么给它一个专家答案并据此打分，本身就是一个相当好的代理。」其后果是，两家拥有不同专家的公司最终会得到不同的模型。失效模式是他真正看过的那种：模型在软件包里找漏洞，以便在编码任务上轻松拿到奖励。「它们发现这事就像水。它们找阻力最小的路径。」

Two structural claims close the episode. The company is sixteen months old and its bet is that “there's a new AI hyperscaler to be built”, on the argument that the inference engine is “the first piece of software that has achieved real stickiness inside of the AI software stack”. And he does not take the one-model-per-firm position the host offers him: not every company needs to pre-train, but “there are closed loop feedback systems inside of each company that is worth capturing”. Whether company-specific models win, on his account, comes down to a gap nobody has measured, between the experts a data vendor can recruit and the in-house experts writing their own evals.

两个结构性主张收尾。这家公司成立十六个月，它的赌注是「有一个新的 AI hyperscaler 值得被建起来」，理由是推理引擎是「AI 软件栈里第一件真正获得了黏性的软件」。而主播递给他的「一家公司一个模型」那个立场，他并没有接下：不是每家公司都需要预训练，但「每家公司内部都有值得捕获的闭环反馈系统」。按他的说法，公司专属模型能不能赢，取决于一个还没有人测过的差距 —— 数据供应商能招募到的专家，与在内部自己写 evals 的专家之间的差距。
