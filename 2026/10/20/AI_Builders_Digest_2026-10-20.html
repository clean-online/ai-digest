Aaron Levie Box CEO

One post at 22:19 Pacific, 121 likes and 13 reposts, and it is the clearest thesis in the window. The claim in his words: “We can finally make compute a critical variable in a large number of important tasks in the economy which wasn’t possible before.” His argument is that a large amount of work is constrained only by the time that goes into research, data processing, discovery and running tests, and that all of it is bottlenecked by planning, executing, reviewing and coordinating, so “we basically do very little of them today relative to their economic value”.

Agent swarms are his answer: “the first real ability to parallelize this work in a way where — in theory — the only parameter that matters is how much compute you throw at the problem.” He lists cyber security, life sciences and material science research, trading, QA testing and data processing, and closes on solving “arbitrarily complex problems by setting the right goals and applying enough tokens to the problem”, which he says changes competitive dynamics in those fields as well.

Nothing is measured. There is no count, no before-and-after, no customer, no benchmark, and “in theory” is his own hedge. It also comes from someone with a position: Levie runs Box, which sells on exactly this account of what AI does to work. That is not a reason to dismiss it, and it is the reason to file it as a position. One more thing is worth noting. “Swarm” appeared in this series two days earlier, in the 10-16 issue, with the opposite valence: Rauch described a white-hat researcher “armed with a swarm of AI agents” piercing the virtualization boundary that cloud sandboxes rely on. Same word, three days apart, one as the thing that breaks in and one as the thing that creates value. Neither measures anything, and they are not about the same object, so this issue does not read them as a dispute.

一条推文，太平洋时间 22:19，121 赞、13 次转发，是这个窗口里最清晰的一个论点。用他的话说，这个主张是：“我们终于可以让算力成为经济中大量重要任务里的一个关键变量，而这在过去是做不到的。”他给出的理由是：有大量工作在本质上只受限于我们投入研究、数据处理、探索和跑测试的时间，而所有这些都被规划、执行、复核和协调卡住，所以“相对于它们的经济价值，我们今天基本只做了其中极少的一部分”。

他的答案是 agent 集群：“第一次真正能把这类工作并行起来，而且 —— 理论上 —— 唯一重要的参数就是你往这个问题上砸多少算力。”他列了网络安全、生命科学与材料科学研究、交易、QA 测试、数据处理，最后落到“设定正确的目标、往问题上投入足够多的 token，就能开始解决任意复杂的问题”，并说这也会改变这些领域里的竞争态势。

没有任何东西被测量过。没有计数，没有前后对比，没有客户，没有基准，而“理论上”是他自己加的限定。它也出自一个在其中有立场的人：Levie 经营 Box，一家正是靠“AI 会怎样改变工作”这套说法来卖产品的公司。这不是否定它的理由，而是该把它归档为“立场”的理由。还有一点值得记下。“集群”这个词在本系列里两天前出现过一次，在 10-16 那期，而且方向正好相反：Rauch 描述的是一位“带着一群 AI agent”的白帽研究者，穿透了云沙箱所依赖的虚拟化边界。同一个词，相隔三天，一个是破门而入的那个东西，一个是创造价值的那个东西。两者都没有测量任何东西，讲的也不是同一个对象，所以这一期不把它们读成一场争论。

The Takeaway

The weight today sits on a remedy rather than a launch. Thibault Sottiaux, who works on Codex and ChatGPT at OpenAI, says OpenAI worked with OpenCode to get affected users running again and to refund credits that “would have been drawn in the attack”, and in the same post offers Daybreak Blue, described as a “cyber permissive setup for defense”, on request. It is the only first-party incident response in this window, and it is also a pitch, since the remedy and the offer arrive in one paragraph. Nothing here discriminates between those two readings, and almost everything a reader would want is missing: the attack has no name, no actor, no date, no user count and no amount.

That gap is the day’s most important fact, and it is worth being precise about why. A vendor reporting that a third party’s users were hit through its own API credits is information regardless of motive, because vendors do not usually volunteer it. But the report is also the only source for it, and it supplies no number that anyone could check, no timeline, and no statement about what was taken. The correct grade is a report from an interested party, and the correct next question is what the incident actually was.

Two unrelated uses of one word are worth holding side by side. In the 10-16 issue Rauch described a white-hat researcher “armed with a swarm of AI agents” piercing the virtualization boundary that cloud sandboxes depend on. Today Levie writes that agent swarms make compute “the only parameter that matters”. Three days apart, one swarm is the thing that breaks in, the other is the thing that creates value. They are not the same object, they cite nothing in common, and neither measures anything, so the useful reading is that the word is doing more work than the evidence behind it.

Two people independently report the same shift, from using a thing to making it, and they grade their own claims very differently. Thariq says building the game and its characters was as much fun as playing it, proposes giving the game away as open source and letting players author the characters, and then says twice that he does not know what it means. Levie says the binding constraint on a large class of work has moved from the work itself to the coordination around it, and states it as a direction rather than a question. One is a preference report from n=1 with the uncertainty left in; the other is a thesis from someone who sells into it. Neither carries a number.

核心结论

这个缺口才是今天最重要的事实，值得把原因说清楚。一家厂商主动说某个第三方的用户是通过自己的 API 额度被波及的，无论动机如何，这本身就是信息，因为厂商通常不会主动讲这种事。但这份报告也是它唯一的来源，而且它没有给出任何可核对的数字、没有时间线、也没有说明有什么被拿走。正确的评级是“利益相关方给出的一份报告”，正确的下一个问题是这次事故到底是什么。

同一个词有两次互不相干的用法，值得摆在一起看。10-16 那期里，Rauch 描述一位“带着一群 AI agent”的白帽研究者穿透了云沙箱所依赖的虚拟化边界。今天 Levie 写的是 agent 集群让算力成了“唯一重要的参数”。相隔三天，一边是破门而入的那个集群，另一边是创造价值的那个集群。它们不是同一个对象，没有共同引用的东西，也都没有测量任何东西，所以更有用的读法是：这个词承担的工作，比它背后的证据要多。

有两个人各自独立地报告了同一个转向，从使用一个东西变成制作一个东西，而他们给自己主张的评级很不一样。Thariq 说做这个游戏和它的角色跟玩它一样好玩，提议把游戏以开源形式送出去、让玩家自己来做角色，然后两次说不知道这意味着什么。Levie 说一大类工作真正的约束已经从工作本身移到了围绕它的协调上，并且是作为一个方向、而不是一个问题说出来的。一个是 n=1 的偏好报告，不确定性留在里面；另一个是出自卖这套东西的人的论点。两者都不带数字。
