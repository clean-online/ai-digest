Aaron Levie Box CEO

One post at 21:44 Pacific, 83 likes, 7 reposts, 46 replies, answering a post that is not in this feed. He takes on the bitter-lesson reading of the applied layer: as models get better, the question is how that intelligence gets diffused into an enterprise, and his answer is that the ability to differentiate domain by domain goes “up instead of down”.

The argument runs on a comparison of two eras. When models were limited, he says, all you could be was a “wrapper”, delivering tokens back to the user behind a custom prompt or some limited RAG. In a world of agents using tools, tasks that run “for hours or days”, workflows that kick off agents, agent swarms coordinated against a goal, domain-specific data sources, model routing that needs domain-specific decisions, and implementation requiring “real change management of the workflow instead of just type into this chat box”, specialization has more room rather than less. He names security, life sciences, healthcare, finance and manufacturing as where autonomous systems for job functions will appear.

Two things to hold it to. It carries no number, no customer, no measurement and no counterexample, so it is an argument from structure and should be read as one. He also supplies his own boundary: “the closer you are to the labs core use-cases the tougher this play gets”. The swarm wording is the third appearance in four issues: on 10-16 Rauch used it on the threat side, on 10-20 Levie used it as the unit of value, and today it is the thing that has to be coordinated against a goal. Three appearances, two of them from the same author, and no shared definition anywhere.

一条推文，太平洋时间 21:44，83 赞、7 次转发、46 条回复，回应的是一条不在这个 feed 里的推文。他接的是应用层上那种 bitter lesson 式的读法：模型越来越强之后，问题变成怎么把这些智能扩散进一家企业，而他的答案是按领域做差异化的能力“不降反升”。

论证靠的是两个时代的对比。他说，模型还很有限的时候，你唯一能做的就是当一个“wrapper”，用一套自定义 prompt 或者一点有限的 RAG 把 token 递回给用户。而在今天这个世界里，agent 在用工具，任务一跑就是“几小时或几天”，工作流会去触发 agent，多个 agent 组成的集群要对着一个目标被协调，数据源是领域专属的，模型路由也得按领域来做决策，落地时需要的不是“往这个聊天框里打字”而是对工作流做真正的变更管理 —— 在这种情况下，做专的空间是变大而不是变小。他点名安全、生命科学、医疗、金融和制造，说我们会看到面向这些岗位职能的自主系统出现。

读它的时候有两点要记住。它没有数字、没有客户、没有测量、也没有反例，所以它是一个结构性的论证，应该被当成结构性论证来读。而且他自己给了边界：“越靠近实验室的核心用例，这套打法越难成立”。swarm 这个词是本系列四期里的第三次出现：10-16 Rauch 拿它讲威胁那一面，10-20 Levie 拿它当价值的单位，今天它是那个要对着一个目标被协调的东西。三次出现，其中两次出自同一位作者，而且没有一次给出过定义。
